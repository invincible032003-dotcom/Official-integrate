@topic Statistical System at the Centre
Q: In the Indian statistical system, collection of statistics for subject areas like agriculture, labour, commerce and industry vests with:
A) the National Statistical Office exclusively
B) the corresponding administrative ministries
C) the Reserve Bank of India
D) the State Directorates of Economics and Statistics only
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: The system is **decentralised by subject**. Statistics on a subject are collected by the **administrative ministry in charge of that subject**, often as a by-product of administration or of monitoring programmes. MoSPI/NSO coordinates and sets standards.
SC: The ministry that administers a subject also collects its statistics.

Q: Consider the following statements:
1. Much statistical information in India is collected as a by-product of administration.
2. All ministries of the Government of India have full-fledged statistical divisions.
3. Ministries such as Agriculture, Water Resources and Health have full-fledged statistical divisions.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 1 and 2 only
B) 1 and 3 only
C) 2 and 3 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: B
T: Statement
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: Statements 1 and 3 are correct. Statement 2 is wrong: **only some** ministries (Agriculture, Water Resources, Health, etc.) have full statistical divisions, and **most others have only a nucleus cell**.
SC: "All ministries" is an absolute claim, so treat it with suspicion. The source says "most others have only a nucleus cell".

Q: In important central ministries, statistical functions are performed by officers of which service along with subordinate statistical staff?
A) Indian Economic Service
B) Indian Statistical Service
C) Indian Administrative Service
D) Central Secretariat Service
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: Officers of the **Indian Statistical Service (ISS)** and the Subordinate Statistical Service (SSS) do the statistical work in ministries. MoSPI is the **cadre-controlling authority** for the ISS.
SC: Statistical work is done by the ISS (officers) and the SSS (subordinate staff).

Q: Which statistical operations are generally centralised in India because they serve the needs of many ministries and the States?
A) Population Census, Annual Survey of Industries and Economic Census
B) Crop-cutting experiments, land-use statistics and livestock census
C) Retail price collection and district statistical handbooks
D) Registration of births and deaths
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: **Large-scale operations** such as the **Population Census, ASI and Economic Census** are centralised. Crop statistics, price collection at the district level and registration of vital events are carried out in a decentralised way through the States.
SC: Big national frames (Census, ASI, EC) are centralised. Routine field and administrative data are decentralised.

@topic Statistical System in the States
Q: At the apex level in a State, which body is formally responsible for coordinating statistical activities?
A) State Planning Department
B) Directorate (formerly Bureau) of Economics and Statistics
C) State Statistical Commission
D) Office of the Chief Secretary
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: The **Directorate of Economics and Statistics (DES)**, earlier called the Bureau of Economics and Statistics, is the apex statistical body of a State. It has statistical offices in the districts and sometimes in regions.
SC: The DES is the State-level counterpart of the CSO/NSO.

Q: Which of the following is NOT a regular activity of the State Directorates of Economics and Statistics?
A) Estimating State Domestic Product
B) Publishing district statistical abstracts
C) Compiling the national Index of Industrial Production
D) Compiling retail price index numbers
ANS: C
T: Odd-one-out
D: Medium
P: 7
EXP: State DESs estimate **SDP**, compile **retail price index numbers**, and publish statistical abstracts, handbooks, economic surveys, district abstracts and budget analyses. The **all-India IIP** is compiled and released monthly by the **NSO (ESD)** at the Centre.
SC: National aggregates (all-India IIP, GDP) belong to the Centre. State aggregates (SDP, State RPI) belong to the DES.

Q: The Statistical System in the States is generally:
A) centralised entirely in the State DES
B) decentralised laterally over the departments of the State Government
C) run directly by the NSO field offices
D) outsourced to the Indian Statistical Institute
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: Like the Centre, a State's system is **decentralised laterally across departments**. Big departments such as agriculture and health have large statistical divisions, and the DES coordinates at the top. Many States have a **common statistical cadre**.
SC: The State system mirrors the Centre: the departments collect and the DES coordinates.

@topic MoSPI: Vision and Mission
Q: Before it became an independent Ministry on 15 October 1999, MoSPI's statistics function existed as:
A) the Planning Commission's Statistics Division
B) the Department of Statistics
C) the Central Statistical Commission
D) the Directorate of Economics and Statistics
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 8
EXP: MoSPI was formed on **15-10-1999** by merging the **Department of Statistics** and the **Department of Programme Implementation**. Before that, statistics was handled by the Department of Statistics.
SC: Department of Statistics + Department of Programme Implementation = MoSPI (1999).

Q: The vision of MoSPI is "to be the best and most innovative National Statistical System in the world; and to ___".
A) publish the largest number of statistical reports
B) effectively monitor programmes and projects for ensuring efficient use of national resources
C) replace administrative statistics with sample surveys
D) centralise all data collection in the Ministry
ANS: B
D: Medium
P: 8
EXP: The vision has two parts that match MoSPI's two wings: the **best and most innovative statistical system** (Statistics Wing) and **effective monitoring of programmes and projects** for efficient use of resources (Programme Implementation Wing).
SC: Two wings give a two-part vision: statistics plus programme monitoring.

Q: Which of the following is part of MoSPI's stated mission?
1. To reduce respondent burden and avoid unnecessary duplication in data collection
2. To maintain confidentiality of data providers and promote integrity and impartiality
3. To participate actively in international initiatives and support statistical systems around the world
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
A) 1 only
B) 1 and 2 only
C) 2 and 3 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: D
T: Statement
D: Medium
P: 8
EXP: All three are part of the MoSPI mission. It also covers timely and reliable statistics, standards and methodologies, leadership and coordination of the NSS, HR capacity building, monitoring of infrastructure projects, and monitoring MPLADS and the Twenty Point Programme.
SC: The mission echoes the UN FPOS (confidentiality, impartiality, cooperation), so all three fit.

Q: Monitoring the implementation of which of the following falls within MoSPI's mission?
A) MPLADS and the Twenty Point Programme
B) MGNREGA and PM-KISAN
C) Mid-Day Meal and ICDS
D) Swachh Bharat and Jal Jeevan Mission
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 9
EXP: The **Programme Implementation Wing** monitors **MPLADS**, the **Twenty Point Programme (TPP)** and **infrastructure/large central projects** (IPMD). The other schemes are run by their own line ministries.
SC: The PI Wing has three areas: MPLADS, TPP and infrastructure projects.
