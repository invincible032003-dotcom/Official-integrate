@topic National Statistical Commission
Q: The present National Statistical Commission was set up on the recommendation of:
A) the Mahalanobis Committee
B) the Rangarajan Commission
C) the Sengupta Committee
D) the Lakdawala Committee
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 14
EXP: The **Rangarajan Commission (2001)** reviewed the Indian statistical system and recommended a permanent **National Statistical Commission**, which was set up by Government Resolution.
SC: Rangarajan reviewed the system in 2001, which led to the NSC in 2005/06.

Q: The National Statistical Commission was set up through a Government Resolution dated ___ and constituted on ___.
A) 1 June 2005; 12 July 2006
B) 12 July 2006; 1 June 2005
C) 15 October 1999; 1 June 2005
D) 1 June 2006; 12 July 2005
ANS: A
T: Chronology
D: Medium
P: 14
EXP: The NSC was **set up by Resolution on 1 June 2005** and **constituted on 12 July 2006**. The resolution must come first, and the constitution followed a year later.
SC: The order is Resolution (2005) then Constitution (2006). Any option with the constitution first is wrong.

@topic Macro-economic Aggregates & Formulae
Q: GVA at basic prices is given by:
A) CE + OS/MI + CFC + Production taxes − Production subsidies
B) CE + OS/MI + CFC + Product taxes − Product subsidies
C) GDP − CFC
D) CE + OS/MI − CFC + Net factor income from abroad
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: **GVA at basic prices = CE + OS/MI + CFC + (production taxes − production subsidies)**. Adding **product taxes net of product subsidies** to GVA at basic prices gives **GDP**.
SC: Basic prices include production taxes. Market prices (GDP) add product taxes on top.

Q: GDP at market prices is obtained from GVA at basic prices by adding:
A) consumption of fixed capital
B) product taxes less product subsidies
C) production taxes less production subsidies
D) net primary income from abroad
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 16
EXP: **GDP = GVA at basic prices + product taxes − product subsidies**. Adding net primary income from abroad gives GNI, and subtracting CFC gives net measures.
SC: Going from GVA (basic) to GDP (market) means adding the per-unit (product) taxes.

Q: Which of the following is a PRODUCTION tax (as distinct from a product tax) as per the national accounts classification?
A) Excise duty
B) Sales tax
C) Stamps and registration fees
D) Import duty
ANS: C
D: Medium
P: 17
EXP: **Production taxes** are paid in connection with production **regardless of the volume of output**, for example **land revenue, stamps and registration fees**. **Product taxes** are levied **per unit** of product: excise, sales tax, service tax, import and export duties.
SC: Ask whether the tax rises with each unit sold. If yes, it is a product tax. If no, it is a production tax.

Q: Match List-I (Item) with List-II (Category):
| List-I | List-II |
|---|---|
| A. Fertiliser subsidy | 1. Production subsidy |
| B. Subsidy to Railways | 2. Product subsidy |
| C. Land revenue | 3. Product tax |
| D. Service tax | 4. Production tax |
A) A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
B) A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
C) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
D) A-1, B-2, C-3, D-4
ANS: A
T: Match
D: Medium
P: 17
EXP: Food, petroleum and **fertiliser subsidies** are **product subsidies** (per unit). Subsidies to **Railways** and village/small industries are **production subsidies**. **Land revenue** is a **production tax**. **Service tax** is a **product tax**.
SC: Per unit means "product". Lump sum or independent of volume means "production".

Q: GNI (GNP) is obtained from GDP by:
A) subtracting consumption of fixed capital
B) adding net primary income from the rest of the world
C) adding other current transfers from abroad
D) subtracting net indirect taxes
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 16
EXP: **GNI = GDP + net primary income from abroad** (receipts minus payments of compensation and property income from the rest of the world). Subtracting CFC gives NNI. Adding current transfers gives disposable income (GNDI).
SC: "National" means adding net income earned abroad (NFIA).

Q: Net National Income at factor cost (NNP at factor cost) is commonly called:
A) Gross Value Added
B) National Income
C) Personal Disposable Income
D) Gross National Disposable Income
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 16
EXP: The source's derivation chart ends with **(NNP)fc = National Income**. Start from GDPmp, subtract depreciation, add NFIA and subtract net indirect taxes.
SC: National Income is Net, National, at Factor cost: N-N-F.

Q: Gross National Disposable Income (GNDI) equals:
A) GNI + other current transfers from the rest of the world (net)
B) GNI − CFC
C) NNI + CFC − current transfers
D) GDP + product taxes
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: **NNDI = NNI + other current transfers from ROW (net)**, and **GNDI = NNDI + CFC = GNI + other current transfers (net)**. Current transfers are items such as remittances and grants, other than primary incomes.
SC: "Disposable" means adding current transfers (remittances) to national income.

Q: As per the identity used in Indian national accounts, Gross Capital Formation equals:
A) Gross Savings + Net capital inflow from the rest of the world
B) GFCF − Changes in stocks
C) Gross Savings − Net capital inflow
D) PFCE + GFCE
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: **GCF = Gross Savings + Net Capital Inflow from ROW**. This is treated as the **firmer** estimate. When GCF is also built up as **GFCF + CIS + valuables**, the difference between the two is recorded as **errors and omissions**.
SC: Investment = domestic saving + foreign saving.

Q: In the national accounts, "errors and omissions" in capital formation arise as the difference between:
A) GDP by the production and expenditure approaches
B) GCF derived from savings plus net capital inflow and the sum of GFCF, CIS and valuables
C) Advance and Provisional estimates
D) GVA at basic prices and GVA at factor cost
ANS: B
D: Hard
P: 17
EXP: The source says GCF from **savings + net capital inflow** is taken as the **firmer** estimate. The gap between it and **GFCF + CIS + valuables** is shown as "**errors and omissions**".
SC: E&O is the balancing item that makes the savings side equal the asset side.

Q: Primary incomes, as used in national accounts, comprise:
A) Compensation of employees and property & entrepreneurial income
B) Current transfers and capital transfers
C) Taxes and subsidies
D) Wages and pensions only
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: **Primary incomes = compensation of employees + property and entrepreneurial income**. These are incomes earned from taking part in production or from owning assets. Transfers are secondary distributions.
SC: Primary income is earned (labour or property). Transfers are given (secondary).

Q: The gross disposable income of the Government equals:
A) GFCE + Gross Saving of General Government
B) PFCE + GFCE
C) GNDI − CFC
D) Taxes − Subsidies
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 16
EXP: Disposable income is either consumed or saved. So **GDI of Government = GFCE + Gross saving of general government**. The GDI of households is found as **GNDI − GDI of Government − Gross savings of all corporations**.
SC: Disposable income = consumption + saving, which holds for any sector.
