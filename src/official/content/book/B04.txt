A) 1 and 2 only
B) 2 and 3 only

SC: India every 5 years, FAO every 10. Agricultural year July-June, forests April-March.

Q: In land-record States, data on the number and area of operational holdings for the Agriculture Census are obtained by:
A) household enquiry on a 20 per cent sample
B) re-tabulation by Patwaris from the land records (Khasra register)
C) satellite imagery only
D) self-reporting by farmers online
ANS: B
D: Medium
P: 22
EXP: In **land-record States**, which cover about **86%** of the reported area, **Patwaris retabulate** holdings from the **Khasra** register. In **non-land-record States**, data come from a **household enquiry** in a sample of villages.
SC: Land-record States use the Patwari and Khasra. Non-land-record States use a sample household survey.

Q: The Agriculture Census is carried out in three phases. Which phase collects data on input use from holdings in about 7 per cent of villages?
A) Phase I
B) Phase II
C) Phase III
D) Phase IV
ANS: C
D: Medium
P: 23
EXP: **Phase I**: listing of holdings (census in land-record States, 20% sample villages in others). **Phase II**: detailed agricultural characteristics from **20% sample villages**. **Phase III (Input Survey)**: input-use patterns from holdings in **7% of villages**, estimated at the district and State level.
SC: Phases 1-2-3 run List → Detail (20%) → Inputs (7%). The sample shrinks as the detail grows.

Q: Effective literacy rate, as used by the Census since 1991, is defined as:
A) (Literates of all ages / Total population) × 100
B) (Literates aged 7+ / Population aged 7+) × 100
C) (Literates aged 15+ / Population aged 15+) × 100
D) (Literates aged 6+ / Population aged 6+) × 100
ANS: B
D: Easy
P: 25
EXP: **Effective literacy rate = (literates aged 7 and above ÷ population aged 7 and above) × 100**. Children aged **0–6 are treated as illiterate** by definition and so are excluded. **Crude literacy rate** divides literates by the **total** population. The adult literacy rate uses 15+.
SC: Census literacy uses 7+ (effective). The 15+ base is the adult literacy rate used internationally.
