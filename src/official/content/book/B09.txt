@topic HLG-MOS Models
Q: MoSPI's transformation approach follows the recommendations of which body?
A) UNECE High-level Group for the Modernisation of Official Statistics (HLG-MOS)
B) IMF Statistics Department
C) OECD Statistics Directorate
D) ILO Department of Statistics
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 113
EXP: The approach follows the **UNECE High-level Group for the Modernisation of Official Statistics (HLG-MOS)**, adapted to India. Its models are **GSBPM, GSIM, CSPA and GAMSO**.
SC: The four "generic" models come from UNECE's modernisation group.

Q: Match List-I (Model) with List-II (Description):
| List-I | List-II |
|---|---|
| A. GSBPM | 1. Information objects flowing in and out of statistical processes |
| B. GSIM | 2. Core business processes to produce statistical outputs |
| C. CSPA | 3. Activities of a statistical organisation, including overarching support processes |
| D. GAMSO | 4. Framework to share and reuse methods and services |
A) A-2, B-1, C-4, D-3
B) A-1, B-2, C-4, D-3
C) A-2, B-1, C-3, D-4
D) A-2, B-4, C-1, D-3
ANS: A
T: Match
D: Medium
P: 113
EXP: **GSBPM** = business **process** model. **GSIM** = **information** model. **CSPA** = **production architecture** for sharing and reuse. **GAMSO** = **activity** model that extends GSBPM with overarching support processes.
SC: The second-last letter tells you the model: P(rocess), I(nformation), A(rchitecture), A(ctivity).

Q: How many phases does the Generic Statistical Business Process Model (GSBPM) have?
A) 8
B) 6
C) 10
D) 4
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 118
EXP: GSBPM has **8 phases**: **Specify needs → Design → Build → Collect → Process → Analyse → Disseminate → Evaluate**. Overarching processes such as quality and metadata management run across all of them.
SC: Specify, Design, Build, Collect, Process, Analyse, Disseminate, Evaluate: eight verbs.

Q: In GSBPM, which phase comes immediately after "Collect"?
A) Process
B) Analyse
C) Build
D) Disseminate
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 118
EXP: The order is Specify needs → Design → Build → **Collect → Process** → Analyse → Disseminate → Evaluate. Sub-processes such as classify & code, validate, edit & impute and calculate weights are part of **Process**.
SC: You must process data before you can analyse it.

Q: According to the Vision document, how should the GSBPM be treated while adapting it?
A) As a process matrix with alternative paths and iterative loops, not a strictly linear model
B) As a strictly sequential linear model
C) As a legal mandate for all ministries
D) As a model applicable only to censuses
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 122
EXP: MoSPI recognises that the **GSBPM is not linear**. Sub-processes need not follow a strict order, and it is treated as a **process matrix** with alternative paths and **iterative loops**. More than **50 countries** use it.
SC: GSBPM is a matrix, not a line.

Q: The Vision document states that data exchange protocols across data systems will use:
A) SDMX and DDI
B) XBRL and JSON only
C) HL7 and FHIR
D) EDIFACT
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 123
EXP: Data and metadata exchange will use **SDMX** (Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange, for aggregate data) and **DDI** (Data Documentation Initiative, for microdata and survey metadata).
SC: SDMX is for aggregates and DDI is for surveys and microdata.
