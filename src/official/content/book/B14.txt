@topic ASI Schedule Form Layout
Q: In the ASI schedule Block C (fixed assets), net value at the closing of the year is derived as:
A) Closing gross value − Depreciation up to year end
B) Opening gross value − Depreciation provided during the year
C) Closing gross value + Depreciation provided during the year
D) Opening gross value + Actual additions
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 230
EXP: Block C columns: gross value (opening 3, revaluation additions 4, actual additions 5, deductions 6, **closing 7 = 3+4+5−6**), depreciation (up to year beginning 8, provided during year 9, adjustment for sold/discarded 10, **up to year end 11 = 8+9−10**), net value (**opening 12 = 3−8**, **closing 13 = 7−11**).
SC: Net value = gross value − accumulated depreciation, at the opening and at the closing.

Q: In ASI Block C, the "sub-total" row (item 8) covers which items?
A) Items 2 to 7 (building to other fixed assets), excluding land and CWIP
B) Items 1 to 9
C) Items 1 to 7
D) Items 3 to 6 only
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 230
EXP: Item 8 is the sub-total of **items 2–7** (building, P&M, transport, computers, pollution control, others), i.e. the **depreciable** assets. **Total (item 10) = item 1 (land) + item 8 + item 9 (CWIP)**.
SC: Land (not depreciable) and CWIP (not yet in use) are kept outside the sub-total.

Q: In ASI Block D, working capital (item 16) is computed as:
A) Total current assets (item 11) − Total current liabilities (item 15)
B) Total inventory − Sundry creditors
C) Cash + Debtors
D) Outstanding loans − Current assets
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 231
EXP: **Item 16 = item 11 (total current assets: inventories + cash + debtors + other current assets) − item 15 (total current liabilities: sundry creditors + short-term loans + other current liabilities)**. Reasons must be given for a negative value.
SC: WC = CA − CL.

Q: In ASI Block D, "outstanding loans" (item 17) are reported:
A) excluding interest but including deposits
B) including accrued interest
C) only short-term loans
D) only bank overdrafts
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 231
EXP: Item 17 is **outstanding loans excluding interest but including deposits**. If interest is included, it must be footnoted.
SC: Loans are reported without interest but with deposits.

Q: In ASI Block E, item 14 "Total cost of production" includes all EXCEPT which of the following?
A) Profit of the unit
B) Wages, bonus, PF contributions and welfare expenses
C) Block F expenses (items 1 to 10)
D) Total inputs of Blocks H and I
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 232
EXP: **Total cost of production = labour costs (E: wages, bonus, PF, welfare) + other expenses (F items 1–10) + indigenous inputs (H-23) + imported inputs (I-7)**. Profit is a residual, not a cost.
SC: Cost of production = labour + other expenses + materials.

Q: In ASI Block H, the units of quantity prescribed for electricity, coal and gas consumed are respectively:
A) KWH, tonne and kg
B) MW, kg and litre
C) KWH, kg and cubic metre
D) units, quintal and tonne
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 234
EXP: Electricity (own generated and purchased): **KWH**. Coal: **tonne**. Gas: **kg**. The ready reference also lists solar energy in KWH and water purchased for fuel in kilolitres.
SC: Electricity in KWH, coal in tonnes, gas in kg.

Q: Block I of the ASI schedule seeks details of how many major imported items separately?
A) Five
B) Ten
C) Three
D) Twenty
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 235
EXP: Block I records the **major five imported items** (serials 1–5), then "other imported items" and the total. Block H records the **major ten** indigenous basic items, and Block J the **first ten** major products.
SC: Ten indigenous inputs, five imported inputs, ten products.

Q: Block L (Energy Conservation) of the ASI schedule asks whether measures have been taken regarding saving of:
A) Electricity, coal, oil and gas
B) Water, land, labour and capital
C) Electricity only
D) Solar, wind, hydro and nuclear
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 236
EXP: Block L has four yes/no indicators: **electrical saving, coal saving, oil saving and gas saving**. Any initiative by the owner counts as "yes", whether or not it relates directly to manufacturing.
SC: EC covers the four energy inputs: electricity, coal, oil and gas.

Q: In ASI scrutiny, basic entries must be thoroughly rechecked when the output/input ratio is less than:
A) 0.5
B) 1.0
C) 2.0
D) 0.1
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 238
EXP: Scrutiny checks include: **output/input < 0.5** (recheck), **distributive expenses above 20% of gross sales** (recheck), **bonus above 20% of wages** (remarks), negative GVA (special check), and resale value below purchase value (reasons).
SC: Remember 0.5 for output/input and 20% for both distributive expenses and bonus.

Q: If the total bonus exceeds what percentage of total wages/salaries, the ASI scrutiny requires suitable remarks in the return?
A) 20%
B) 10%
C) 33%
D) 50%
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 238
EXP: A **bonus of more than 20% of wages** is flagged and needs remarks. The same 20% threshold applies to **distributive expenses relative to gross sales**.
SC: Both scrutiny thresholds are 20%.

Q: In ASI scrutiny Part A, "Average salaries/wages per manday worked" is computed from Block E as:
A) wages (col 8) ÷ mandays worked (col 5) for workers
B) wages ÷ number of workers
C) total emoluments ÷ 365
D) bonus ÷ mandays paid
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 237
EXP: Scrutiny item 4(1): **average salaries/wages per manday worked = E5.8 ÷ E5.5**, i.e. total workers' wages divided by total mandays worked. It is compared with the previous year to find anomalies.
SC: The wage rate is money per manday worked.

Q: ASI Part-II (Mandays worked, absenteeism and labour turnover) records data for each month from:
A) April of the reference year to March of the next year
B) January to December
C) July to June
D) Only the survey months
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 240
EXP: Part-II Block 2 has rows for **April 2020 to March 2021** (for ASI 2020-21), i.e. the financial year. For **seasonal industries**, only the months when the main product was made are covered.
SC: Part-II follows the financial year month by month.

@topic Ready Reference: Fixed Assets (Block C)
Q: As per the ASI ready reference, a fencing or compound wall that is external to the factory building is recorded against:
A) Block C, item 1 (Land)
B) Block C, item 2 (Building)
C) Block C, item 7 (Others)
D) Block F, repair and maintenance
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 241
EXP: The **external** fencing or compound wall counts as **land improvement (C-1)**. If it is an **integral part of the factory building**, it goes to **building (C-2)**. Roads, culverts and external sewerage are also C-1.
SC: External works improve land (C-1). Integral works are part of the building (C-2).

Q: Godowns, showrooms, hospital buildings and R&D buildings of a factory are recorded in ASI under:
A) Block C, item 2 (Building)
B) Block C, item 7 (Others)
C) Block D
D) Outside the purview of ASI
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 241
EXP: Every kind of building owned by the factory (factory, office, godown, showroom, hospital, R&D, staff flats) goes under **C-2 Building**.
SC: Any structure is a building (C-2).

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, a DG set, dies & moulds and transformers used for plant & machinery are recorded under:
A) Block C, item 3 (Plant & Machinery)
B) Block C, item 7 (Others)
C) Block C, item 6 (Pollution control)
D) Block H (consumable stores)
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 242
EXP: **DG sets, dies and moulds, furnaces, molasses tanks, sewing machines and transformers for P&M** are **C-3**. **Transformers not for P&M** go to C-7.
SC: If it drives or is part of production machinery, it is P&M.

Q: Electrostatic precipitators, venturi scrubbers and bag-house fabric filters are recorded in ASI Block C under:
A) Item 6: Pollution control/environment improvement equipment
B) Item 3: Plant & machinery
C) Item 7: Others
D) Item 5: Computer equipment
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 243
EXP: **Item 6** covers pollution control equipment: afterburners, biofilters, carbon absorbers, catalytic reactors, cyclones, **ESPs**, **fabric filters**, incinerators, packed towers, spray chambers, thermal oxidisers and **venturi scrubbers**.
SC: Filters, scrubbers and precipitators all fall under pollution control (C-6).

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, electric fans, fire extinguishers, library and laboratory apparatus of a factory are recorded under:
A) Block C, item 7 (Other fixed assets)
B) Block C, item 3 (Plant & machinery)
C) Block C, item 2 (Building)
D) Block F
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 244
EXP: Office and utility items (fans, room ACs and coolers, fire extinguishers, library, lab equipment, furniture, PABX, refrigerators not essential for the product, typewriters, water purifiers) go under **C-7 Others**.
SC: Utilities and office equipment go to "Others" (C-7).

Q: Livestock used for manufacturing, as listed in the ASI ready reference, is recorded under:
A) Block C, item 7 (Others)
B) Block H (raw material)
C) Block D (inventory)
D) Outside the purview of ASI
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 244
EXP: The ready reference lists **livestock used for manufacturing** under **C-7 (other fixed assets)**. The animals serve for more than a year as capital. A cart with animals for transport is recorded under transport equipment (C-4).
SC: Working animals used for more than a year are fixed assets.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, which of the following is "outside the purview of ASI" (not recorded in Block C)?
A) Copyrights and trademarks, or land/building in another State with economic activity
B) Capital work in progress
C) Computer software
D) Pollution control equipment
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 246
EXP: Outside the purview: **copyrights, trademarks, goodwill** and **land or buildings in another State where economic activity is carried out** (a separate production unit). Land or buildings in another State **with no** economic activity are recorded in C-1/C-2.
SC: Intangibles and out-of-State units that are active are excluded.

@topic Ready Reference: Working Capital (Block D)

B) Block D, item 6 (Finished goods)
C) Block D, item 3 (Spares & stores)
D) Block F, item 11
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 246
EXP: **Stock of trading goods** goes to **D-1**, together with raw materials, packing materials, construction material, material in transit (paid for) and materials supplied to others for processing.

Q: Stationery, canteen grain stock and vehicle spares held in stock are recorded in ASI Block D under:

D: Medium
P: 246
EXP: **D-3 (Spares, stores & others)** covers consumable stores, spares, stationery, canteen stock, stock of building material, dead stock and vehicle spares.
SC: Anything that is not raw material, fuel or output goes in D-3.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, "advance to supplier" and "debit balances of sundry creditors" are recorded under:
A) Block D, item 9 (Sundry debtors)
B) Block D, item 12 (Sundry creditors)

ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 247
EXP: **Sundry debtors (D-9)** include advances to customers, parties and suppliers, debit balances of sundry creditors and trade receivables.
SC: Any amount owed to the factory by trade parties is D-9.

Q: Prepaid insurance, interest receivable and earnest money deposited for less than one year are recorded in ASI under:
A) Block D, item 10 (Other current assets)
B) Block D, item 8 (Cash in hand & at bank)
C) Block C
D) Block G
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 248
EXP: **D-10** includes prepaid expenses, interest receivable, short-term earnest money and security deposits (under 1 year), duty drawback receivable, export incentives receivable, loans to staff and advance income tax.
SC: Short-term receivables that are not trade debtors go in D-10.

Q: "Advance from customer" is recorded in ASI Block D under:
A) item 12: Sundry creditors
B) item 9: Sundry debtors
C) item 14: Other current liabilities
D) item 17: Outstanding loans
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 249
EXP: **Advances received from customers and parties are sundry creditors (D-12)**, because the factory owes goods against them.
SC: Money received in advance is a creditor.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, "current maturities of long-term loans", "bonus payable" and "PF payable" are recorded under:
A) Block D, item 14 (Other current liabilities)
B) Block D, item 17 (Outstanding loans)
C) Block D, item 13 (Short-term loans)
D) Block E
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 249
EXP: **D-14 (other current liabilities)** covers bills payable, bonus, ESI and PF payable, current maturities of long-term loans, expenses payable, provisions (short term), taxes payable and unpaid wages.
SC: Short-term payables that are not trade creditors or bank credit go in D-14.

Q: A bank overdraft and working capital loan are recorded in ASI Block D under:
A) item 13: Overdraft, cash credit, other short-term loans
B) item 17: Outstanding loans
C) item 12: Sundry creditors
D) item 14: Other current liabilities
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 249
EXP: **D-13** covers bank overdraft, cash credit, working capital loans, short-term loans from banks and FIs, and short-term loans from directors.
SC: Short-term bank credit is D-13.

Q: Which of the following is recorded under Block D, item 17 (outstanding loans) as per the ASI ready reference?
A) Vehicle loan, term loans from banks (excluding interest) and loans from shareholders
B) Bank overdraft
C) Advance from customers
D) Bills payable
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 251
EXP: **D-17** covers **long-term** borrowings: term loans from banks and FIs (excluding interest), debentures, loans from shareholders and partners, loans from subsidiaries, FDs, and vehicle loans.
SC: Term and long-duration borrowings go in D-17.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, which of the following is "outside the purview of ASI"?
A) Deferred tax assets, bad debts and accrued interest
B) Bank overdraft
C) Sundry debtors
D) Finished goods
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 251
EXP: Items **outside the purview** include accrued interest, advances for purchase or sale of fixed assets, authorised or called-up capital, bad debts, deferred tax assets and liabilities, deferred revenue expenditure, partners' drawings, goodwill and reserves.
SC: Capital-account, reserve and accounting-adjustment items are excluded.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, "materials supplied by others to the factory for processing" are:
A) outside the purview of ASI (not counted in the factory's inventory)
B) recorded in Block D, item 1
C) recorded in Block H
D) recorded in Block J
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 252
EXP: The factory does not own these materials, so they are **outside the purview** of its inventory. Only the **service charges** earned are recorded (G-1).
SC: Goods not owned are not counted as inventory.

@topic Ready Reference: Labour Cost (Block E)
Q: As per the ASI ready reference, which of the following is recorded under wages & salary (Block E, column 8)?
A) Leave encashment, notice pay and income tax paid for employees
B) Contribution to EPF
C) Canteen cost
D) Year-end bonus
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 253
EXP: **Wages & salary (E col. 8)** include basic pay, DA, HRA, overtime, leave encashment, leave salary, LTA, notice pay, retaining and suspension allowance, stipend, production incentive and **income tax paid for employees**. EPF goes in E-11, canteen in E-12, and year-end bonus in E-10.
SC: Wages = everything paid to the individual regularly.

D) Block F, item 3
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 254
EXP: **E-11** covers EPF/CPF, ESI, EDLIS/DLIF, superannuation, group gratuity fund, labour welfare fund and compensation for work injuries.
SC: Employer contributions to funds go in E-11.

Q: As per the ASI ready reference, "bus hired to and from duty", "canteen cost" and "water purchased for drinking of staff" are recorded under:
A) Block E, item 12 (Workmen & staff welfare expenses)
B) Block F, item 3 (Operating expenses)
C) Block H (consumable stores)

ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 254
EXP: **E-12 (welfare)** covers group benefits in kind: canteen, food and beverages, hired bus, LTC, medical, recreation, sports and **drinking water for staff**.
SC: Group benefits in kind go to welfare (E-12).

Q: A uniform provided to employees as a welfare measure is recorded under Block E (welfare), whereas a uniform for carrying out a specific activity/operation is recorded under:
A) Block F, item 3 (Operating expenses)

EXP: The ready reference distinguishes a **uniform as welfare (E-12)** from a **uniform needed for a specific operation (F-3)**. The manual also treats mandatory working-hour uniforms as operating expenses.

EXP: The ready reference lists **CETP charges**, car and computer repairs and repair and maintenance of P&M under **F-2(ii)**, repair and maintenance of **other fixed assets**. Building repairs go to F-2(i).

SC: F-6 is machines and other assets, F-8 is buildings, F-9 is land and royalty.
