@topic Biodiversity & Soil Indices
Q: The Shannon-Wiener index of diversity is given by:
A) H' = −Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ, where pᵢ is the proportion of individuals of species i
B) H' = Σ pᵢ²
C) H' = number of species / total individuals
D) H' = 1 − Σ pᵢ
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 313
EXP: **H' = −Σ pᵢ ln pᵢ**, with **pᵢ = nᵢ/N**. Every ln pᵢ is negative, so the minus sign makes H' positive. It reflects both **richness** (number of species) and **evenness** (how evenly individuals are spread). **Σpᵢ²** is Simpson's index.
SC: Shannon uses p ln p. Simpson uses p².

Q: A community consisting of only one species has a Shannon-Wiener index value of:
A) 0
B) 1
C) 4
D) Infinity
ANS: A
T: Numerical
D: Easy
P: 313
EXP: With one species, **p = 1** and **ln 1 = 0**, so **H' = 0**. In most ecological studies H' lies between **1.5 and 3.5** and rarely goes above **4**.
SC: A single species gives zero diversity.

Q: For a community with two species of equal abundance (p1 = p2 = 0.5), the Shannon-Wiener index is approximately:
A) 0.693
B) 0.5
C) 1.0
D) 0.25
ANS: A
T: Numerical
D: Medium
P: 313
EXP: H' = −2 × 0.5 × ln 0.5 = −ln 0.5 = **ln 2 ≈ 0.693**. For S equally abundant species, H' = ln S, which is the maximum for that richness.
SC: With equal abundance, H' = ln(number of species).

Q: The Shannon index increases as:
A) both richness and evenness of the community increase
B) the dominance of one species increases
C) the number of individuals of one species increases
D) evenness decreases
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 313
EXP: H' **rises with richness and with evenness**. Dominance by one species lowers evenness and so lowers H'.
SC: More species, more evenly spread, gives a higher H'.

Q: The Soil Nutrient Index (N.I.) developed by ICAR is computed as:
A) (N_L × 1 + N_M × 2 + N_H × 3) / N_T
B) (N_L + N_M + N_H) / 3
C) N_H / N_T × 100
D) (N_H − N_L) / N_T
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 313
EXP: **N.I. = (N_L×1 + N_M×2 + N_H×3) ÷ N_T**, where N_L, N_M and N_H are the numbers of samples in the low, medium and high classes and N_T is the total number of samples. It is a weighted average score between 1 and 3.
SC: Weights 1-2-3 for low-medium-high, divided by the total number of samples.

Q: A Soil Nutrient Index value of 2.1 for a nutrient indicates soil fertility status that is:
A) Medium
B) Low
C) High
D) Very high
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 314
EXP: **N.I. below 1.67 = low**. **1.67 to 2.33 = medium**. **Above 2.33 = high**. So 2.1 is **medium**.
SC: The cut-offs are 1.67 and 2.33, i.e. the index range 1-3 split into thirds.

Q: 100 soil samples are classified as 30 low, 50 medium and 20 high for nitrogen. The nutrient index is:
A) 1.90 (medium)
B) 2.50 (high)
C) 1.50 (low)
D) 2.00 (medium)
ANS: A
T: Numerical
D: Medium
P: 314
EXP: N.I. = (30×1 + 50×2 + 20×3) ÷ 100 = (30 + 100 + 60) ÷ 100 = **1.90**, which falls between 1.67 and 2.33, so fertility is **medium**.
SC: Weighted average of 1/2/3 by sample counts.

@topic IUCN & Red List
Q: The International Union for Conservation of Nature (IUCN) was established in:
A) 1948
B) 1961
C) 1972
D) 1992
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 314
EXP: **IUCN was founded in 1948**. It has **over 1,500 member organisations** (States, NGOs, indigenous peoples' organisations, academic bodies) from **160+ countries**. WWF dates from 1961, UNEP from 1972 and the CBD from 1992.
SC: IUCN 1948, the oldest global environmental body.

Q: How many categories are there in the IUCN Red List Categories (global)?
A) Nine
B) Six
C) Seven
D) Eleven
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 314
EXP: The **nine** categories are Extinct (EX), Extinct in the Wild (EW), Critically Endangered (CR), Endangered (EN), Vulnerable (VU), Near Threatened (NT), Least Concern (LC), Data Deficient (DD) and Not Evaluated (NE). **Regional** assessments add Not Applicable and Regionally Extinct.
SC: EX, EW, then the threatened trio CR/EN/VU, then NT, LC, DD, NE: nine in all.

Q: Species in which IUCN Red List categories are collectively described as "threatened"?
A) Critically Endangered, Endangered and Vulnerable
B) Endangered, Vulnerable and Near Threatened
C) Extinct, Extinct in the Wild and Critically Endangered
D) Near Threatened and Least Concern
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 315
EXP: **CR + EN + VU = threatened**. **Near Threatened** is not threatened. Critically Endangered species may also be tagged **Possibly Extinct**.
SC: Threatened means the three categories CR, EN and VU.

Q: Species are assessed against how many criteria for placement into IUCN threatened categories?
A) Five
B) Three
C) Seven
D) Nine
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 315
EXP: The **five criteria (A–E)** are **population reduction**, **restricted geographic range**, **small population size and decline**, **very small or restricted population**, and **quantitative extinction probability analysis**.
SC: A-E: reduction, range, small population, very small population, probability.

Q: Which of the following is NOT included in the IUCN Red List?
A) Not Evaluated species
B) Data Deficient species
C) Least Concern species
D) Near Threatened species
ANS: A
D: Medium
T: Odd-one-out
P: 315
EXP: The Red List **does not include "Not Evaluated"** species, which have not been assessed. Data Deficient, LC and NT species have been evaluated and are listed.
SC: Not evaluated means not on the list.

@topic FDES & Climate Change Statistics
Q: The Framework for the Development of Environment Statistics (FDES) 2013 comprises how many components?
A) Six
B) Four
C) Five
D) Eight
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 316
EXP: FDES 2013 has **6 components**: (1) environmental conditions and quality, (2) environmental resources and their use, (3) residuals, (4) extreme events and disasters, (5) human settlements and environmental health, (6) environmental protection, management and engagement.
SC: Conditions, Resources, Residuals, Extremes, Settlements, Protection: six.

Q: FDES 2013 is designed to be compatible with which of the following frameworks?
1. SEEA
2. DPSIR (Driving force-Pressure-State-Impact-Response)
3. SDG and sustainable development indicator frameworks
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
A) 1 only
B) 1 and 2 only
C) 2 and 3 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: D
T: Statement
D: Medium
P: 316
EXP: FDES 2013 links to economic and social domains and is compatible with **SEEA, DPSIR, the MDGs/SDGs and SDI frameworks**. Where possible it uses existing statistical classifications.
SC: FDES links to all of these, so all three are correct.

Q: The UNFCCC defines climate change as a change of climate:
A) attributed directly or indirectly to human activity altering the global atmosphere, in addition to natural climate variability
B) caused only by solar cycles
C) caused only by volcanic eruptions
D) lasting less than one year
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 317
EXP: The general definition includes **natural and anthropogenic** causes lasting decades or more. The **UNFCCC** definition covers only change **attributed to human activity** that alters atmospheric composition, **over and above natural variability**.
SC: The UNFCCC counts only human-caused change beyond natural variability.

@topic PSUT, Protected Areas, CBD & CITES
Q: Physical Supply and Use Tables (PSUT) record flows of natural inputs, products and residuals in physical units where:
A) rows show types of natural inputs, products and residuals, and columns reflect activities and economic units
B) rows show economic units and columns show prices
C) only monetary values are recorded
D) only stocks are recorded
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 321
EXP: In the PSUT, **rows = types of flows** (natural inputs, products, residuals) and **columns = activities and economic units** (plus the environment). Supply tables record origins and use tables record destinations. Monetary SUTs cover the same flows in money terms.
SC: Rows are what flows. Columns are who supplies or uses it.

Q: As per IUCN, "National Park" corresponds to which Protected Area category?
A) Category II
B) Category Ia
C) Category IV
D) Category VI
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 322
EXP: IUCN categories: **Ia/Ib** strict nature reserve and wilderness area, **II national park**, **III** natural monument, **IV** habitat/species management area, **V** protected landscape/seascape, **VI** protected area with sustainable use.
SC: Ia/Ib wild, II park, III monument, IV habitat, V landscape, VI sustainable use.

Q: In India, Protected Areas declared under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972 fall into which four categories?
A) National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves and Community Reserves
B) Biosphere Reserves, Tiger Reserves, Ramsar Sites and Elephant Reserves

ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 322
EXP: Under the **WPA 1972**, the four PA categories are **National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves and Community Reserves**. Conservation and Community Reserves were added by the 2002 amendment. Biosphere reserves and Ramsar sites are not WPA categories.
SC: NP + WLS + CR + CommR.

Q: Which of the following is NOT one of the three main objectives of the Convention on Biological Diversity?
A) Complete ban on the use of genetic resources
B) Conservation of biological diversity
C) Sustainable use of its components
D) Fair and equitable sharing of benefits from utilisation of genetic resources
ANS: A
D: Easy
T: Odd-one-out
P: 323
EXP: The CBD's three objectives are **conservation**, **sustainable use**, and **fair and equitable benefit-sharing** from genetic resources (later made operational through the Nagoya Protocol). It does not ban use.
SC: Conserve, use sustainably, share benefits. No bans.

Q: CITES entered into force on:
A) 1 July 1975
B) 3 March 1973
C) 29 December 1993
D) 5 June 1972
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 323
EXP: CITES was drafted after a **1963 IUCN resolution**, **signed in 1973** (Washington, 3 March), and **entered into force on 1 July 1975**. It regulates international trade in endangered species.
SC: IUCN 1963 → signed 1973 → in force 1975.

Q: Under CITES, species threatened with extinction, in which international commercial trade is generally prohibited, are listed in:
A) Appendix I
B) Appendix II
C) Appendix III
D) Appendix IV
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 323
EXP: **Appendix I**: threatened with extinction, commercial trade prohibited except in exceptional cases (for example research) with import and export permits. **Appendix II**: not necessarily threatened but trade must be controlled (export permit). **Appendix III**: protected in at least one country that has asked others for help.
SC: I = banned, II = controlled, III = one country's request.
