@topic Global Bodies
Q: The United Nations Statistical Commission, the apex body of the global statistical system, was established in:
A) 1946, as a functional commission of ECOSOC
B) 1945, under the UN Charter as a principal organ
C) 1961, under the UN General Assembly
D) 1972, under UNDP
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **UN Statistical Commission (1946)** is a **functional commission of ECOSOC** with **24 member States** elected for **4 years**. It meets annually in **New York**, and **UNSD** is its secretariat.
SC: UNSC was set up in 1946 under ECOSOC, with 24 members and UNSD as secretariat.

Q: The UN Statistical Commission consists of how many member States?
A) 24
B) 15
C) 54
D) 193
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: There are **24 members**, elected by **ECOSOC** on an equitable geographical basis for **4-year terms**. ECOSOC itself has 54 members, which is the distractor.
SC: UNSC has 24 members; ECOSOC has 54.

Q: The International Statistical Institute (ISI), one of the oldest international statistical bodies, was founded in 1885 and is headquartered at:
A) The Hague, Netherlands
B) Geneva, Switzerland
C) Kolkata, India
D) New York, USA
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **ISI (1885)** is based in **The Hague**. It organises the **World Statistics Congress** and adopted a **Declaration on Professional Ethics** (1985). The **Indian Statistical Institute (Kolkata, 1931)** is a different body.
SC: International ISI is in The Hague (1885); Indian ISI is in Kolkata (1931).

Q: The International Association for Official Statistics (IAOS) is a specialised association of:
A) the International Statistical Institute
B) the IMF
C) the World Bank
D) the OECD
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **IAOS (1985)** is a **section of the ISI** devoted to official statistics. It publishes the **Statistical Journal of the IAOS**.
SC: IAOS is the official-statistics wing of the ISI.

Q: The Statistical Institute for Asia and the Pacific (SIAP), a training institute of ESCAP, is located at:
A) Chiba, Japan
B) Bangkok, Thailand
C) Manila, Philippines
D) New Delhi, India
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **SIAP** (set up **1970**) is in **Chiba, Japan** and trains official statisticians of the region. **ESCAP** itself is headquartered in **Bangkok**, and the **ADB** in Manila.
SC: SIAP is in Chiba; ESCAP is in Bangkok.

Q: The Committee for the Coordination of Statistical Activities (CCSA) coordinates statistical work among:
A) international and supranational organisations
B) Indian States
C) Central Ministries of India
D) private data firms
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **CCSA** coordinates the statistical programmes of **international organisations** (UN agencies, IMF, World Bank, OECD, Eurostat and others). Within India, coordination is done by **MoSPI/NSO** and forums such as **COCSSO**.
SC: CCSA coordinates international agencies; COCSSO coordinates Indian States.

Q: The UN World Data Forum was first held in 2017 at Cape Town, where the following was launched:
A) Cape Town Global Action Plan for Sustainable Development Data
B) Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics
C) SDDS Plus
D) SEEA Central Framework
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **first UN World Data Forum (Cape Town, January 2017)** launched the **Cape Town Global Action Plan** for sustainable development data, focused on building statistical capacity for the SDGs.
SC: Cape Town 2017 produced the Global Action Plan for SDG data.

Q: The Inter-agency and Expert Group on SDG Indicators (IAEG-SDGs) is responsible for:
A) developing and reviewing the global indicator framework for the SDGs
B) funding national statistical offices
C) compiling India's NIF
D) ranking Indian States on SDGs
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **IAEG-SDGs**, under the UN Statistical Commission, develops and refines the **global indicator framework** and classifies indicators into **tiers**. India's **NIF** is compiled by **MoSPI**, and State rankings come from **NITI Aayog**.
SC: IAEG-SDGs designs global indicators; MoSPI adapts them into the NIF.

Q: Under the SDG indicator tier system, a Tier II indicator is one that:
A) has an established methodology and standards, but data are not regularly produced by countries
B) has no internationally established methodology
C) has both methodology and regular data from most countries
D) is used only at the national level
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **Tier I**: methodology exists and data are regularly produced. **Tier II**: methodology exists but data are not regular. **Tier III**: no established methodology. Tier III was emptied by 2020.
SC: Tier I has method and data; Tier II method only; Tier III neither.

@topic IMF & World Bank Standards
Q: Arrange the following IMF data dissemination initiatives in chronological order of launch:
1. SDDS Plus
2. SDDS
3. e-GDDS
4. GDDS
A) 2-4-1-3
B) 4-2-1-3
C) 2-4-3-1
D) 2-1-4-3
ANS: A
T: Chronology
D: Hard
EXP: **SDDS 1996 → GDDS 1997 → SDDS Plus 2012 → e-GDDS 2015** (enhanced GDDS replaced GDDS). India subscribed to **SDDS in 1996**.
SC: 96 SDDS, 97 GDDS, 2012 Plus, 2015 e-GDDS.

Q: The IMF's SDDS Plus, the highest tier of its data standards, was introduced in:
A) 2012
B) 1996
C) 2015
D) 2008
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **SDDS Plus (2012)** adds data categories such as sectoral balance sheets and financial soundness indicators, for systemically important economies. India subscribes to **SDDS**.
SC: SDDS Plus dates from 2012, after the global financial crisis.

Q: The International Comparison Program (ICP), which produces purchasing power parities (PPPs) to compare economies, is globally coordinated by:
A) the World Bank
B) the IMF
C) the WTO
D) the OECD alone
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **ICP** is coordinated by the **World Bank**, with regional agencies such as the ADB for Asia-Pacific. India participates through **NSO (PSD)**. In PPP terms, India ranks as the world's **third-largest economy** in recent ICP rounds.
SC: ICP means World Bank PPPs; in India it is handled by PSD.

Q: Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) between two countries is best defined as:
A) the number of units of a country's currency required to buy the same basket of goods and services that one unit of a reference currency buys in the reference country
B) the market exchange rate
C) the ratio of two countries' GDPs
D) the ratio of two countries' CPI inflation rates
ANS: A
T: Conceptual
D: Medium
EXP: A **PPP** is a **spatial price deflator and currency converter**. It equalises purchasing power across countries, unlike **market exchange rates**, which reflect traded goods and capital flows.
SC: PPP makes a basket cost the same everywhere.

Q: The Living Standards Measurement Study (LSMS), a household survey programme, is associated with:
A) the World Bank
B) UNICEF
C) USAID
D) the ILO
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **LSMS** is run by the **World Bank** (since 1980). **MICS** is UNICEF's programme and **DHS** is USAID's, with NFHS as India's DHS. **Labour force survey** standards come from the **ILO**.
SC: LSMS is the World Bank's, MICS UNICEF's, DHS USAID's.

Q: Match List-I (Survey programme) with List-II (Sponsoring agency):
| List-I | List-II |
|---|---|
| A. MICS | 1. USAID |
| B. DHS | 2. World Bank |
| C. LSMS | 3. UNICEF |
A) A-3, B-1, C-2
B) A-1, B-3, C-2
C) A-2, B-1, C-3
D) A-3, B-2, C-1
ANS: A
T: Match
D: Medium
EXP: The matches are **MICS → UNICEF**, **DHS → USAID** (NFHS is India's DHS) and **LSMS → World Bank**.

Q: PARIS21, which promotes National Strategies for the Development of Statistics (NSDS), is hosted by:
A) OECD
B) UNSD
C) Eurostat
D) IMF
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **PARIS21** (Partnership in Statistics for Development in the 21st Century), created in **1999**, is hosted by the **OECD** in Paris. It champions **NSDS** in developing countries.
SC: PARIS21 is Paris-based at the OECD and promotes the NSDS.

@topic ILO & Labour Standards
Q: The 19th International Conference of Labour Statisticians (2013) is known for:
A) a new framework of work statistics, restricting "employment" to work for pay or profit
B) defining the informal sector for the first time
C) the International Classification of Status in Employment 2018
D) the definition of hours actually worked
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **19th ICLS (2013)** set out **forms of work**: **employment (work for pay or profit)**, **own-use production work**, unpaid trainee work, volunteer work and other work. The informal sector was defined by the **15th ICLS (1993)**, **ICSE-18** came from the **20th ICLS (2018)**, and working time from the **18th ICLS**.
SC: 15th informal sector, 17th informal jobs, 18th working time, 19th forms of work, 20th ICSE-18.

Q: ICSE-18, the International Classification of Status in Employment, was adopted by:
A) the 20th ICLS in 2018
B) the 15th ICLS in 1993
C) the UN Statistical Commission in 2008
D) the 19th ICLS in 2013
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **ICSE-18** replaced **ICSE-93**. It was adopted at the **20th ICLS (2018)** and introduced the **dependent contractor** category.
SC: ICSE-18 comes from the 20th ICLS in 2018.

Q: Which of the following is correctly matched?
A) 15th ICLS – Informal sector
B) 17th ICLS – Status in employment (ICSE-18)
C) 18th ICLS – Forms of work framework
D) 20th ICLS – Informal employment
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The correct pairs are **15th (1993) → informal sector**, **17th (2003) → informal employment**, **18th (2008) → working time**, **19th (2013) → forms of work** and **20th (2018) → ICSE-18**.
SC: The ICLS runs 15-17-18-19-20 as sector, jobs, time, work forms, status.

@topic Classifications
Q: India's National Industrial Classification NIC-2008 is aligned with:
A) ISIC Revision 4
B) ISIC Revision 3.1
C) NAICS
D) SITC Revision 4
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **NIC-2008** follows **ISIC Rev.4** (UN, 2008). **NIC-2004** followed **ISIC Rev.3.1**, and **NIC-1998** followed **ISIC Rev.3**.
SC: NIC-08 ↔ ISIC 4, NIC-04 ↔ ISIC 3.1, NIC-98 ↔ ISIC 3.

Q: Match List-I (International classification) with List-II (Indian counterpart / use):
| List-I | List-II |
|---|---|
| A. ISCO-08 | 1. NPCMS |
| B. CPC | 2. ITC-HS |
| C. Harmonized System | 3. NIC |
| D. ISIC | 4. NCO |
A) A-4, B-1, C-2, D-3
B) A-1, B-4, C-2, D-3
C) A-4, B-2, C-1, D-3
D) A-4, B-1, C-3, D-2
ANS: A
T: Match
D: Medium
EXP: The pairs are **ISCO → NCO** (occupations), **CPC → NPCMS** (products), **HS → ITC-HS** (traded goods, 8-digit) and **ISIC → NIC** (industries).
SC: Occupation NCO, product NPCMS, trade ITC-HS, industry NIC.

Q: The Harmonized Commodity Description and Coding System (HS) is maintained by:
A) the World Customs Organization
B) the WTO
C) UNCTAD
D) the UN Statistics Division
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **HS** is maintained by the **World Customs Organization (WCO)**. It is harmonised internationally at **6 digits** and revised roughly every **5 years** (current edition HS 2022). India's **ITC-HS** extends it to **8 digits**.
SC: HS is WCO's, 6 digits worldwide; India uses 8.

Q: The International Standard Classification of Education (ISCED 2011) is maintained by:
A) UNESCO (UNESCO Institute for Statistics, Montreal)
B) the World Bank
C) UNICEF
D) OECD
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **ISCED** belongs to **UNESCO**. The **UNESCO Institute for Statistics (UIS)** in **Montreal** is the custodian of education SDG data.
SC: Education classification is UNESCO's, via UIS in Montreal.

Q: The WHO's ICD-11 (International Classification of Diseases, 11th revision) came into effect on:
A) 1 January 2022
B) 1 January 2016
C) 1 January 2020
D) 1 April 2019
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **ICD-11** came into effect on **1 January 2022**, replacing ICD-10. Cause-of-death coding such as India's **MCCD** has long used **ICD-10**.
SC: ICD-11 took effect on 1 January 2022.

Q: COICOP is the international classification used for:
A) household consumption expenditure by purpose
B) government functions
C) industries
D) occupations
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **COICOP** is the Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose. It is used for **PFCE** and **CPI** groupings. Government functions use **COFOG**.
SC: COICOP is for households; COFOG is for government.

Q: Which UN classification groups traded goods into categories such as capital goods, intermediate goods and consumption goods for economic analysis?
A) Broad Economic Categories (BEC)
B) ISIC
C) ISCO
D) ICD
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **BEC** reclassifies trade (HS/SITC) into **end-use** categories: **capital, intermediate and consumption** goods. **SITC** (Standard International Trade Classification) groups goods by **stage of processing**.
SC: BEC classifies trade by end use.

Q: India's National Classification of Occupations, NCO-2015, is based on:
A) ISCO-08
B) ISCO-88
C) ISIC Rev.4
D) ICSE-93
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **NCO-2015** (Directorate General of Employment, MoLE) is aligned with **ISCO-08**. **NCO-2004** followed **ISCO-88**.
SC: NCO-15 ↔ ISCO-08; NCO-04 ↔ ISCO-88.

@topic Accounting Standards & Models
Q: Arrange the editions of the UN System of National Accounts in chronological order:
1. SNA 1993
2. SNA 1968
3. SNA 2008
4. SNA 1953
A) 4-2-1-3
B) 2-4-1-3
C) 4-1-2-3
D) 4-2-3-1
ANS: A
T: Chronology
D: Easy
EXP: The editions run **1953 → 1968 → 1993 → 2008**. India's **2011-12 series** follows **SNA 2008**. A **2025 SNA** has since been adopted by the UN Statistical Commission.
SC: 53, 68, 93, 2008, then 2025.

Q: The IMF's Balance of Payments and International Investment Position Manual, 6th edition (BPM6), was released in:
A) 2009
B) 1993
C) 2014
D) 2001
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **BPM6** (2009) is harmonised with **SNA 2008**. The **RBI** compiles India's BoP on BPM6. Related IMF manuals include the **GFSM 2014** (government finance) and the **MFSM** (monetary and financial statistics).
SC: BPM6 dates from 2009 and matches SNA 2008.

Q: The Generic Statistical Business Process Model (GSBPM) is developed under the auspices of:
A) UNECE (High-Level Group for the Modernisation of Official Statistics)
B) the IMF
C) the World Bank
D) the ILO
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **GSBPM**, along with **GSIM, CSPA and GAMSO**, comes from **UNECE's HLG-MOS**. It describes the phases of statistical production, from **specifying needs to evaluation**.
SC: The generic models all come from UNECE's HLG-MOS.

Q: SDMX, an ISO standard for exchanging statistical data and metadata, is sponsored by:
A) BIS, ECB, Eurostat, IMF, OECD, UN and World Bank
B) only the UNSD
C) only the IMF
D) national statistical offices of G20 countries
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **SDMX** is sponsored by **seven international organisations**: BIS, ECB, Eurostat, IMF, OECD, UN and World Bank. It is used for exchanging aggregate data, for example with the IMF's NSDP. **DDI** is used for microdata documentation.
SC: SDMX handles aggregates between agencies; DDI documents microdata.

@topic Principles & Ethics
Q: Which of the following was adopted by the International Statistical Institute in 1985?
A) Declaration on Professional Ethics
B) Fundamental Principles of Official Statistics
C) SDDS
D) Cape Town Global Action Plan
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **ISI Declaration on Professional Ethics** (1985, revised 2010) guides statisticians' obligations to society, funders, colleagues and subjects. The **FPOS** were adopted by the **UNSC in 1994**.
SC: ISI ethics 1985; UN FPOS 1994.

Q: Consider the following statements about international statistical coordination:
1. UNSD serves as the secretariat of the UN Statistical Commission.
2. Eurostat is the statistical office of the European Union, located in Luxembourg.
3. The OECD is headquartered in Geneva.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
A) 2 and 3 only
B) 1 and 2 only
C) 1 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: B
T: Statement
D: Medium
EXP: **UNSD (New York)** is the secretariat of the UNSC. **Eurostat** is in **Luxembourg**. The **OECD** is headquartered in **Paris**, not Geneva, which is home to the ILO and WHO.
SC: UNSD New York, Eurostat Luxembourg, OECD Paris, ILO/WHO Geneva.

Q: Which organisation compiles the World Development Indicators (WDI)?
A) World Bank
B) UNDP
C) IMF
D) WTO
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **WDI** are the **World Bank's** flagship database. The **Human Development Report** is **UNDP's**. The IMF publishes the **World Economic Outlook**, and the WTO the **World Trade Statistical Review**.
SC: WDI is the World Bank's; HDR is UNDP's; WEO is the IMF's.

Q: Which of the following international statistical publications is correctly matched with its agency?
A) World Economic Outlook – IMF
B) Human Development Report – World Bank
C) World Development Report – UNDP
D) Global Hunger Index – FAO
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The correct pairs are **WEO → IMF**, **HDR → UNDP**, **WDR → World Bank** and **GHI → Welthungerhilfe & Concern Worldwide**. The FAO publishes **SOFI**, the State of Food Security and Nutrition.
SC: WEO is the IMF's, HDR UNDP's, WDR the World Bank's, GHI from NGOs.

Q: The FAO's World Programme for the Census of Agriculture is conducted in rounds of:
A) ten years
B) five years
C) one year
D) twenty years
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **World Census of Agriculture** runs **decennially**, as in the WCA 2020 round covering 2016-2025. **India** conducts its Agriculture Census **every 5 years** within that programme.
SC: The FAO round is 10 years; India holds its census every 5.

Q: The UN Global Geospatial Information Management initiative (UN-GGIM) primarily aims to:
A) integrate geospatial information with statistics and strengthen national geospatial capacity
B) set price index standards
C) compile trade statistics
D) regulate satellite launches
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **UN-GGIM** (2011) supports geospatial capacity and, with the Statistical Commission, the **Global Statistical Geospatial Framework**, which links statistics to location.
SC: GGIM brings together maps and statistics.

Q: The Statistical Performance Indicators (SPI), which replaced the Statistical Capacity Index, are published by:
A) the World Bank
B) the UNSD
C) PARIS21
D) the IMF
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **World Bank's SPI** (2021) scores countries on **five pillars**: data use, data services, data products, data sources and data infrastructure. It replaced the older **Statistical Capacity Index**.
SC: The World Bank's SPI replaced its capacity index.

Q: Which of the following statements about purchasing power parities is correct?
A) GDP in PPP terms usually raises the relative size of developing economies compared with market exchange rates
B) PPPs are identical to market exchange rates
C) PPPs are compiled by the WTO
D) PPPs ignore non-traded services
ANS: A
T: Conceptual
D: Medium
EXP: **Non-traded services are cheaper** in developing countries, so **PPP-based GDP is larger** relative to market-rate GDP. This is why India ranks **3rd in PPP** terms.
SC: Cheap local services inflate PPP GDP for developing countries.

Q: The Global Hunger Index is jointly published by:
A) Welthungerhilfe and Concern Worldwide
B) FAO and WFP
C) UNICEF and WHO
D) World Bank and IFAD
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **GHI** is published by **Welthungerhilfe** (Germany) and **Concern Worldwide** (Ireland). Its indicators are undernourishment, **child stunting**, **child wasting** and **under-5 mortality**.
SC: GHI comes from two NGOs, not a UN agency.
