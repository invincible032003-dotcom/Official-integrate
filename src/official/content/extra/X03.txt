@topic MoSPI Publications
Q: The publication "Women and Men in India" is brought out by:
A) Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation
B) Ministry of Women and Child Development
C) National Commission for Women
D) Office of the Registrar General of India
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **"Women and Men in India"** is MoSPI's (NSO, SSD) regular compendium of **gender statistics**, covering population, health, education, participation in the economy and decision-making. MoSPI also publishes **Children in India**, **Elderly in India** and **Youth in India**.
SC: The gender compendium is MoSPI's (SSD).

Q: Which of the following thematic publications are brought out by MoSPI?
1. Elderly in India
2. Youth in India
3. Children in India
4. Crime in India
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
A) 1, 2, 3 and 4
B) 1 and 3 only
C) 2, 3 and 4 only
D) 1, 2 and 3 only
ANS: D
T: Statement
D: Easy
EXP: **Elderly, Youth and Children in India** are MoSPI (SSD) compendiums. **Crime in India** is published by the **NCRB (MHA)**.
SC: "... in India" population-group reports are MoSPI's; crime is NCRB's.

Q: "Energy Statistics India", which presents the energy balance of the country, is published annually by:
A) NSO, MoSPI
B) Central Electricity Authority
C) Ministry of Petroleum and Natural Gas
D) Bureau of Energy Efficiency
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **Energy Statistics India** (NSO, MoSPI; annual) compiles reserves, production, consumption and the **energy balance** across coal, oil, gas, electricity and renewables. The **CEA** publishes electricity-sector statistics, and **MoPNG** publishes Indian Petroleum & Natural Gas Statistics.
SC: The all-energy balance is MoSPI's; single-sector data come from line ministries.

Q: EnviStats India, published annually in two volumes (environment statistics and environmental accounts), is brought out by:
A) NSO, MoSPI
B) Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change
C) Central Pollution Control Board
D) Forest Survey of India
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **EnviStats India** comes from **NSO (SSD), MoSPI**. **Vol. I** covers environment statistics on the **FDES** framework; **Vol. II** covers **environmental-economic accounts (SEEA)**. The **India State of Forest Report** comes from the **FSI**.
SC: EnviStats is MoSPI's: Vol I statistics, Vol II accounts.

Q: "Statistical Year Book India" and "India in Figures" are publications of:
A) MoSPI
B) Ministry of Finance
C) NITI Aayog
D) RBI
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: Both are **MoSPI** compilations. The **Year Book** contains **sector-wise tables** and **India in Figures** is a **pocket digest**. The **Economic Survey** is the **Ministry of Finance's**.
SC: The Year Book and India in Figures are MoSPI's.

Q: The annual "National Indicator Framework Progress Report" on SDGs is released by:
A) MoSPI
B) NITI Aayog
C) UNDP India
D) Ministry of External Affairs
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **MoSPI** publishes the **NIF Progress Report**, measuring India's progress on national SDG indicators. **NITI Aayog** publishes the **SDG India Index**, ranking States and UTs.
SC: MoSPI measures progress; NITI ranks the States.

Q: The "Payroll Reporting in India: An Employment Perspective" release is made by:
A) NSO, MoSPI, monthly
B) EPFO, quarterly
C) Labour Bureau, annually
D) RBI, half-yearly
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **NSO** releases payroll data (new subscribers to **EPF, ESI, NPS**) **monthly**, with the notes placing it around the 25th. The underlying data come from EPFO, ESIC and PFRDA.
SC: Payroll reporting is NSO's monthly compilation of EPF, ESI and NPS data.

@topic RBI & Finance Publications
Q: The "Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy" is published annually by:
A) Reserve Bank of India
B) MoSPI
C) Ministry of Finance
D) NITI Aayog
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **RBI** publishes the **Handbook of Statistics on Indian Economy** and the **Handbook of Statistics on Indian States** (both annual), the **RBI Bulletin** (monthly), and the **Report on Trend and Progress of Banking**.
SC: The Handbook of Indian Economy statistics is the RBI's.

Q: The Financial Stability Report of the RBI is published:
A) half-yearly
B) monthly
C) annually
D) quarterly
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **FSR is half-yearly** (June and December). The **Monetary Policy Report** is also **half-yearly**. The **RBI Bulletin** is **monthly**. The **Annual Report** and **Report on Currency and Finance** are annual or occasional.
SC: FSR and MPR come out twice a year; the Bulletin monthly.

Q: The Economic Survey of India, presented before the Union Budget, is prepared by:
A) Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance (under the Chief Economic Adviser)
B) NITI Aayog
C) RBI
D) MoSPI
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **Economic Survey** is prepared by the **Economic Division of DEA**, led by the **Chief Economic Adviser**, and tabled before the Budget. It draws on **MoSPI and RBI data**.
SC: The Survey comes from the CEA's team in DEA, before the Budget.

Q: "Indian Public Finance Statistics" is published by:
A) Ministry of Finance
B) RBI
C) CAG
D) Finance Commission
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: **Indian Public Finance Statistics** (annual) comes from the **Ministry of Finance** (DEA). The **RBI's "State Finances: A Study of Budgets"** covers State budgets.
SC: IPFS is from the Ministry of Finance; State budget studies from the RBI.

@topic Sectoral Ministries & Agencies
Q: "Agricultural Statistics at a Glance" is an annual publication of:
A) Directorate of Economics and Statistics, Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare
B) NSO, MoSPI
C) ICAR
D) NABARD
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **DES, MoA&FW** publishes **Agricultural Statistics at a Glance** and the **Pocket Book of Agricultural Statistics**. **Horticultural Statistics at a Glance** is also from MoA&FW.
SC: Agriculture compendiums come from DES, MoA&FW.

Q: "Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics" is published by:
A) Department of Animal Husbandry and Dairying
B) DES, Ministry of Agriculture
C) NSO
D) National Dairy Development Board
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying** publishes **Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics** (annual) and conducts the **Livestock Census**. Fisheries statistics come from the **Department of Fisheries**.
SC: Livestock data come from DAHD.

Q: The "Indian Labour Journal" (monthly) and the "Indian Labour Year Book" are published by:
A) Labour Bureau
B) Directorate General of Employment
C) EPFO
D) NSO
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **Labour Bureau** (Shimla/Chandigarh, MoLE) publishes the **Indian Labour Journal** (monthly, with CPI-IW figures), the **Indian Labour Year Book**, and statistics on strikes, lockouts and wages.
SC: Labour periodicals come from the Labour Bureau.

Q: The "National Health Profile", an annual compendium of health statistics, is published by:
A) Central Bureau of Health Intelligence (CBHI)
B) ICMR
C) IIPS, Mumbai
D) National Health Authority
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **CBHI** (under **DGHS, MoHFW**) publishes the **National Health Profile**. **IIPS** conducts the **NFHS**. **ICMR** is a research council.
SC: The National Health Profile comes from CBHI.

Q: The annual publication on health infrastructure and human resources in rural and urban areas, earlier titled "Rural Health Statistics", is now titled:
A) Health Dynamics of India (Infrastructure & Human Resources)
B) National Health Accounts
C) Health Management Information System Report
D) Family Welfare Statistics in India
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: MoHFW's **Rural Health Statistics** was renamed **Health Dynamics of India (Infrastructure & Human Resources)**, covering sub-centres, PHCs, CHCs and staff. **HMIS** is the web-based facility reporting system.
SC: Rural Health Statistics is now called Health Dynamics of India.

Q: The Unified District Information System for Education Plus (UDISE+) is maintained by:
A) Department of School Education & Literacy, Ministry of Education
B) NCERT
C) UGC
D) NSO
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **UDISE+** is the school-level data system of the **Department of School Education & Literacy**. **AISHE** (All India Survey on Higher Education) comes from the **Department of Higher Education**. The **National Achievement Survey** comes from **NCERT**.
SC: UDISE+ covers schools, AISHE higher education, NAS learning outcomes.

Q: The All India Survey on Higher Education (AISHE) has been conducted annually since:
A) 2010-11
B) 2001-02
C) 2015-16
D) 1991-92
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: **AISHE** has been conducted annually since **2010-11** by the **Ministry of Education (Department of Higher Education)**. It gives **GER in higher education** (ages 18-23), institutions, enrolment and teachers.
SC: AISHE runs from 2010-11; its GER uses ages 18-23.

Q: "Crime in India", "Accidental Deaths & Suicides in India" and "Prison Statistics India" are published by:
A) National Crime Records Bureau
B) Bureau of Police Research & Development
C) Central Bureau of Investigation
D) MoSPI
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **NCRB (MHA)** publishes all three annually. **BPR&D** deals with police research and modernisation.
SC: Crime, ADSI and prison data all come from NCRB.

Q: "Road Accidents in India" is an annual publication of:
A) Ministry of Road Transport and Highways
B) NCRB
C) NHAI
D) MoSPI
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **MoRTH (Transport Research Wing)** publishes **Road Accidents in India** and the **Road Transport Year Book**. The NCRB's **ADSI** also reports traffic accidents, using police data.
SC: The road accidents report comes from MoRTH.

Q: "India Tourism Statistics" is published by:
A) Ministry of Tourism
B) MoSPI
C) Ministry of External Affairs
D) Bureau of Immigration
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **Ministry of Tourism** publishes **India Tourism Statistics** (foreign tourist arrivals, domestic tourist visits, foreign exchange earnings). MoSPI's **NSS** conducted the **Domestic Tourism** surveys (e.g. the 78th round).
SC: Tourism statistics come from the Ministry of Tourism; household tourism surveys from NSS.

Q: The "Indian Minerals Yearbook" is published by:
A) Indian Bureau of Mines
B) Geological Survey of India
C) Ministry of Coal
D) DGMS
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **Indian Bureau of Mines (Nagpur)** publishes the **Indian Minerals Yearbook** and supplies mining data for the **IIP**. The **GSI** does geological mapping and exploration.
SC: IBM Nagpur publishes the minerals yearbook.

Q: The "India State of Forest Report" is published:
A) biennially by the Forest Survey of India
B) annually by MoEFCC
C) every five years by ISRO
D) quarterly by the FAO
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **ISFR** is **biennial**, from the **Forest Survey of India (Dehradun)**. It assesses forest and tree cover using **remote sensing** (since **1987**).
SC: The forest report comes every 2 years from FSI.

Q: "Monthly Statistics of Foreign Trade of India" is brought out by:
A) DGCI&S, Kolkata
B) DGFT
C) RBI
D) Ministry of External Affairs
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **DGCI&S** compiles merchandise trade from customs data. The **RBI** compiles the **balance of payments**, and the **DGFT** handles licensing.
SC: Trade volumes come from DGCI&S; BoP from the RBI.

Q: Vital Statistics of India based on the Civil Registration System is published by:
A) Office of the Registrar General of India
B) Ministry of Health and Family Welfare
C) NSO
D) State DESs
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **ORGI (MHA)** publishes the **CRS report** (annual), the **SRS Statistical Report and Bulletins**, and the **MCCD report**.
SC: CRS, SRS and MCCD reports all come from ORGI.

Q: "All India Electricity Statistics – General Review" is published by:
A) Central Electricity Authority
B) Power Finance Corporation
C) Bureau of Energy Efficiency
D) NSO
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **CEA** (under the Ministry of Power) publishes the **General Review** on electricity generation, consumption and losses. It is also the **source of electricity data for IIP**.
SC: Electricity statistics come from the CEA.

Q: The Coal Directory of India is published by:
A) Coal Controller's Organisation
B) Coal India Limited
C) Indian Bureau of Mines
D) Central Mine Planning & Design Institute
ANS: A
D: Hard
EXP: The **Coal Controller's Organisation** (Ministry of Coal) compiles **coal statistics** and the **Coal Directory**. **IBM** covers **non-coal** minerals.
SC: Coal data come from the Coal Controller; other minerals from IBM.

Q: Which of the following is released monthly?
A) Index of Eight Core Industries
B) Annual Survey of Industries
C) Economic Census
D) Agriculture Census
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **ICI** (OEA) is **monthly**, as are IIP, CPI and WPI. **ASI** is annual, the **EC** periodic, and the **Agriculture Census** quinquennial.
SC: Indices are monthly; censuses are periodic.

D) Road Transport Year Book – MoRTH
ANS: A

D: Easy
EXP: **Sarvekshana** is the **NSO's** journal (the NSS journal, since 1977). The other three pairs are correct.

Q: The Economic Census, which counts all non-farm establishments, has been conducted by:
A) MoSPI (earlier CSO, now NSO), with States/UTs
B) Office of the Registrar General of India
C) Ministry of MSME
D) Ministry of Commerce
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **EC** is MoSPI's (**ESD**). The **7th EC (2019-20)** was carried out through **CSC e-Governance Services**. The **Population Census** is ORGI's.

Q: Which organisation publishes the "Global Gender Gap Report"?
A) World Economic Forum
B) UNDP
C) UN Women
D) World Bank
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The **Global Gender Gap Index** (WEF, since 2006) covers four sub-indices: **economic participation & opportunity, educational attainment, health & survival, and political empowerment**. **UNDP** publishes the **GDI and GII** in its Human Development Report.
SC: GGGI is from WEF; GDI and GII are from UNDP.

Q: The "World Happiness Report" is published by:
A) UN Sustainable Development Solutions Network (with Gallup data)
B) UNDP
C) WHO
D) OECD
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **World Happiness Report** is published by the **UN SDSN** with partners, using **Gallup World Poll** life evaluations. The **OECD** has a separate **Better Life Index**.
SC: The happiness report comes from SDSN and Gallup.

Q: The "Global Innovation Index" is published by:
A) World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO)
B) WTO
C) UNCTAD
D) World Economic Forum
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The **GII** is published by **WIPO** (with Portulans Institute and partners). **UNCTAD** publishes the **World Investment Report**.
SC: The innovation index is WIPO's; investment reports are UNCTAD's.

Q: Monitoring of the Twenty Point Programme (TPP) and the release of funds under MPLADS are functions of:
A) the Programme Implementation Wing of MoSPI
B) NITI Aayog
C) the Ministry of Rural Development
D) the Lok Sabha Secretariat
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: MoSPI's **PI Wing** monitors the **Twenty Point Programme**, administers **MPLADS** and, through **IPMD**, tracks **Central Sector projects of ₹150 crore and above**.
SC: TPP, MPLADS and project monitoring all sit in MoSPI's PI Wing.

Q: The "Annual Report on Periodic Labour Force Survey" and the quarterly PLFS bulletins are released by:
A) NSO, MoSPI
B) Labour Bureau
C) Ministry of Labour and Employment
D) EPFO
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **PLFS** is the **NSO's** survey, with fieldwork by **FOD**. The **Labour Bureau** runs **QES/AQEES** and the other establishment surveys.
SC: PLFS belongs to NSO; QES to the Labour Bureau.

Q: Which of the following is the correct pairing of index and its compiling agency?
A) CPI-AL/RL – Labour Bureau; CPI (Combined) – NSO; WPI – OEA, DPIIT
B) CPI-AL/RL – NSO; CPI (Combined) – Labour Bureau; WPI – RBI
C) CPI-AL/RL – DES, MoA&FW; CPI (Combined) – RBI; WPI – NSO
D) All three are compiled by NSO
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: The compilers are **Labour Bureau** for **CPI-IW, CPI-AL and CPI-RL**, **NSO (PSD)** for **CPI-R/U/C**, and **OEA, DPIIT** for **WPI** and **ICI**. **NSO (ESD)** compiles **IIP**.
SC: The Labour Bureau handles worker CPIs, NSO the general CPI, OEA the WPI.

Q: The "Report on Trend and Progress of Banking in India" is an annual statutory publication of:
A) RBI
B) Indian Banks' Association
C) Ministry of Finance (Department of Financial Services)
D) NABARD
ANS: A
D: Medium
EXP: The RBI submits this report to the Government under the **Banking Regulation Act, 1949**. It reviews the banking sector's performance and policies.

Q: Under the IMF SDDS, MoSPI publishes an advance release calendar. The purpose of an advance release calendar is to:
A) announce release dates of data in advance, ensuring equal access and transparency
B) delay the release of sensitive data
C) permit revision without notice
D) restrict data to Government users first
ANS: A
T: Conceptual
D: Easy
EXP: The **ARC** announces dates in advance. It supports **FPOS Principle 1 (equal access)** and **transparency**, and prevents selective early access.
SC: An advance calendar gives everyone access at the same time.

Q: Which agency compiles the "Index of Industrial Production"?
A) NSO (ESD), MoSPI
B) OEA, DPIIT
C) RBI
D) CEA
ANS: A
D: Easy
EXP: **IIP** is compiled by **NSO (ESD)** from **14 source agencies**, the main one being **DPIIT**. The **OEA** compiles the **ICI and WPI**.
