@topic NSO: Mandate
Q: Which of the following is NOT a responsibility of the National Statistical Office?
A) Compiling the Wholesale Price Index
B) Preparing national accounts and comparable SDP estimates
C) Compiling the monthly IIP quick estimates
D) Conducting the Economic Census and follow-up enterprise surveys
ANS: A
T: Odd-one-out
D: Easy
P: 2
EXP: The NSO prepares **national accounts** and comparable **SDP**, compiles the **IIP**, runs the **ASI**, the **Economic Census** and follow-up surveys, and conducts large sample surveys. The **WPI** is compiled by the **Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT**.
SC: WPI comes from the OEA in the Commerce Ministry.

Q: The NSO coordinates statistical work of Ministries and advises them on methodology. It coordinates at the State level with:
A) State Statistical Bureaus (SSBs)/DESs
B) District Collectors
C) Panchayati Raj institutions
D) State Election Commissions
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 1
EXP: NSO coordinates with Central Ministries and **State Statistical Bureaus (now the State DESs)**, and advises on statistical methodology and analysis.
SC: The Centre's counterpart in each State is the SSB/DES.

Q: With which of the following international organisations does the NSO maintain liaison?
1. UNSD and ESCAP
2. SIAP and IMF
3. ADB, FAO and ILO
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
A) 1 only
B) 1 and 2 only
C) 2 and 3 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: D
T: Statement
D: Easy
P: 2
EXP: The notes list **UNSD, ESCAP, SIAP, IMF, ADB, FAO and ILO**. All three groups are correct.
SC: All the statistical and development agencies are included.

Q: The NSO releases grants-in-aid to registered NGOs and research institutions for:
A) special studies/surveys, printing statistical reports and financing seminars on official statistics
B) running schools
C) rural employment works
D) infrastructure construction
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 3
EXP: Mandate item 10: **grants-in-aid** for **special studies or surveys**, **printing statistical reports** and **seminars, workshops and conferences** on official statistics.
SC: Grants go to statistical research and outreach.

Q: Which NSO responsibility involves examining survey reports from the technical angle and evaluating sampling designs of surveys by other Central Ministries?
A) Technical evaluation of survey designs and feasibility studies
B) Grants-in-aid
C) Dissemination
D) Economic Census
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 2
EXP: Mandate item 8: NSO **examines survey reports technically** and **evaluates sampling designs**, including feasibility studies, for surveys by the NSSO and other Ministries. It acts as the system's technical reviewer.
SC: NSO acts as the peer reviewer of government surveys.

B) Survey Coordination Division (SCD)
C) Field Operations Division (FOD)
D) Data Quality Assurance Division (DQAD)
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 3
EXP: **SDRD (Kolkata)** does survey planning, sample design, schedules, concepts, tabulation plans, analysis and **survey reports**. It is **ISO:9000 certified**.

Q: The Data Quality Assurance Division (DQAD) of NSO:
A) is headquartered at Kolkata, has six Data Processing Centres and houses the Industrial Statistics Wing

SC: DQAD = old DPD + IS Wing, in Kolkata.

SC: SCD is the coordinator and publisher, in Delhi.

@topic Chief Statistician of India

Q: The post of Chief Statistician of India was first held from:
A) 21 February 2007
B) 15 October 1999
C) 12 July 2006
D) 1 June 2005
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 4
EXP: The CSI post was created alongside the NSC. **Pronab Sen** took office on **21 February 2007**. The NSC was constituted on 12 July 2006.
SC: The NSC was constituted in 2006 and the first CSI took office in 2007.

Q: Prof. T.C.A. Anant served as Chief Statistician of India from 2010 to:
A) January 2017
B) June 2010
C) August 2020
D) March 2014
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 4

SC: Anant served 2010-2017.

@topic Statistics Day & World Statistics Day
Q: In which year was National Statistics Day first celebrated in India?
A) 2007
B) 1993
C) 2010
D) 1999
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 4
EXP: **Statistics Day (29 June)**, Mahalanobis's birthday, was **first celebrated in 2007**. It aims to spread awareness, especially among young people, about the role of statistics in planning.
SC: The first Statistics Day was in 2007, the same year the first CSI took office.

Q: The theme of National Statistics Day 2022 was:
A) Data for Sustainable Development
B) Better data, better lives
C) Connecting the world with data we can trust
D) SDGs
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 4
EXP: Themes per the notes: **2019 – SDGs**; **2020 – SDG 3 and SDG 5**; **2021 – SDG 2 (Zero Hunger)**; **2022 – Data for Sustainable Development**. The other options are World Statistics Day themes.
SC: The 2022 theme is Data for Sustainable Development. The WSD themes use the "data we can trust" style.

Q: National Statistics Day 2021 focused on which SDG?
A) SDG 2 – End hunger, food security and nutrition, sustainable agriculture
B) SDG 5 – Gender equality
C) SDG 13 – Climate action
D) SDG 16 – Peace and justice
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 4
EXP: The **2021** theme was **SDG 2**. The **2020** theme combined **SDG 3 (health) and SDG 5 (gender)**.
SC: 2020 covered SDGs 3 and 5. 2021 covered SDG 2.

Q: World Statistics Day is celebrated on 20 October every:
A) five years
B) year
C) two years
D) ten years
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 4
EXP: **World Statistics Day (20 October)** is celebrated **every five years**: **2010** (first), **2015** and **2020**. It follows UNGA resolution 64/267.
SC: WSD is on 20.10, every 5 years: 2010, 2015, 2020.

@topic The 12 Divisions of NSO
Q: Which NSO division is the nodal division for the Collection of Statistics Act, 2008 and organises COCSSO and Statistics Day?
A) Coordination and Publication Division (CPD)
B) Survey Coordination Division
C) Social Statistics Division
D) Policy Implementation and Monitoring Division
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 5
EXP: **CPD** coordinates with Ministries and State DESs. It implements the SSS (Support for Statistical Strengthening) scheme and World Bank projects, is the **nodal division for the CoS Act**, **organises COCSSO and Statistics Day**, monitors the CD scheme, and handles parliamentary and RTI matters.
SC: CPD handles the "C" tasks: Coordination, CoS Act, COCSSO.

Q: Which NSO divisions are stated to be ISO:9000 certified in the notes?
A) NAD, PSD and SDRD
B) FOD, DQAD and SCD
C) SSD, ESD and CPD
D) DIID, PIMD and Training
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 5
EXP: The notes mark **NAD, PSD and SDRD** as **ISO:9000**, consistent with the Vision 2024 document.
SC: The certified trio is National accounts, Prices and Survey design.

Q: The Data Informatics and Innovation Division (DIID) of NSO:
A) develops application software, uses the IHSN toolkit, provides NIC cloud (MeghRaj) services, transforms data into SDMX and is developing NIIP and DI-Lab
B) conducts field surveys
C) compiles the CPI
D) coordinates the SDG NIF
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 5
EXP: **DIID** (erstwhile Computer Centre) handles MoSPI's software, the **IHSN microdata toolkit**, **MeghRaj (NIC cloud)**, **SDMX** conversion, **NIIP** and the **DI-Lab**.
SC: DIID is MoSPI's IT and data-innovation division.

Q: The Price Statistics Division (PSD) of NSO:
A) compiles CPI (Rural/Urban/Combined) and State-wise CPI, and coordinates the International Comparison Programme
B) compiles WPI and CPI-IW
C) compiles IIP and ISP
D) compiles terms of trade
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 6
EXP: **PSD** (ISO 9000) compiles **CPI (R/U/C)** monthly, **State-wise CPI** and **CFPI**, and coordinates the **ICP** (PPPs). WPI is compiled by the OEA-DPIIT and CPI-IW by the Labour Bureau.
SC: PSD handles CPI and PPP (ICP).

Q: Which NSO division compiles the monthly IIP, energy statistics, NIC/NPC and conducts the Economic Census?
A) Economic Statistics Division (ESD)
B) National Accounts Division
C) Price Statistics Division
D) Survey Design and Research Division
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 7
EXP: **ESD** covers the **IIP** (monthly, about a 6-week lag), **Energy Statistics** (annual), **NIC and NPC**, the **Economic Census** (7th in 2019-20) and the **Index of Service Production**.
SC: ESD is the "economic" division: industry, energy, classifications and the EC.

Q: Which NSO division was created with effect from January 2021 to devise data governance strategies and ensure use of Industry 4.0 technologies?
A) Policy Implementation and Monitoring Division (PIMD)
B) Data Informatics and Innovation Division
C) Coordination and Publication Division
D) Training Division
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 7
EXP: **PIMD**, **new from January 2021**, is mandated to improve **data governance**, steer the action plan for **Industry 4.0 technologies**, lead **inter-ministerial statistical liaison** and develop a **quality framework** to international standards.
SC: PIMD (2021) is the governance and Industry 4.0 division.

A) numerical data collected, compiled and published by different government bodies
B) data published by private research firms
C) only census data
D) data used by courts
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 1
EXP: The notes define **official statistics** as **numerical data collected, compiled and published by government bodies**. They are a public good.

Q: The NSO prepares comparable estimates of State Domestic Product at:
A) current prices
B) constant prices only
C) factor cost of 1950-51
D) purchasing power parity
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 2
EXP: The mandate says NSO prepares **comparable estimates of SDP at current prices**, along with national product, consumption, capital formation, savings and capital stock estimates.
SC: The mandate refers to comparable SDP at current prices.

Q: IIP quick estimates are compiled and released by the NSO at what frequency?
A) Every month
B) Every quarter
C) Every year
D) Every five years
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 2
EXP: The NSO releases **IIP quick estimates every month**, with a roughly 6-week lag, and conducts the **ASI** annually.
SC: IIP is monthly.

Q: The NSO disseminates data, on request, to which UN agencies, as per its mandate?
A) UNSD, ESCAP, ILO and others
B) UNHCR and UNICEF only
C) WHO only
D) UN Security Council
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 3
EXP: Mandate item 9: disseminate publications to government and private users, and data on request to **UNSD, ESCAP, ILO** and other international agencies.
SC: The statistical UN bodies are UNSD, ESCAP and ILO.
