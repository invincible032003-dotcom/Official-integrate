@topic Price Indices
Q: The base year of the Consumer Price Index for Agricultural and Rural Labourers (CPI-AL/RL) is:
A) 1986-87
B) 2012
C) 2016
D) 2011-12
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 10
EXP: **CPI-AL/RL: base 1986-87** (Labour Bureau). **CPI (R/U/C): 2012** (NSO-PSD). **CPI-IW: 2016** (Labour Bureau). **WPI and IIP: 2011-12**.
SC: AL/RL 1986-87, R/U/C 2012, IW 2016, WPI/IIP 2011-12.

Q: CPI-AL is used for which of the following purposes?
1. Fixation and revision of minimum wages in the agricultural sector
2. Moving base-year poverty lines for rural areas to subsequent years
3. Calculating Dearness Allowance of Central Government employees
Select the correct answer using the code given below:
A) 1 and 2 only
B) 2 and 3 only
C) 1 and 3 only
D) 1, 2 and 3
ANS: A
T: Statement
D: Medium
P: 10
EXP: **CPI-AL** is used for **agricultural minimum wages** and for **updating rural poverty lines**. **DA of Government employees** is based on **CPI-IW**, which is also used for updating urban poverty lines.
SC: AL is for rural wages and rural poverty. IW is for DA and urban poverty.

P: 10

Q: Which statement about the Wholesale Price Index (WPI) is correct?
A) It is compiled monthly by the Office of the Economic Adviser, DPIIT, with base 2011-12, and covers goods only, not services

Q: Why was the Index of Service Production (ISP) initiated by ESD, NSO?
A) Because IIP and ASI cover only manufacturing items and electricity, while ISP covers services such as banking, transport and telecom
B) To replace the WPI
C) To measure agricultural output

@topic Development Indices
Q: Which of the following indices uses "Estimated earned income (male and female) per capita" as its standard of living indicator?
A) Gender Development Index (GDI)
B) Human Development Index (HDI)
C) Gender Inequality Index (GII)
D) Global Hunger Index (GHI)
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 13
EXP: The **GDI** computes HDI separately for females and males, using **estimated earned income by sex**. The **HDI** uses GNI per capita (the notes use per capita GSDP for sub-national HDI). The **GII** uses reproductive health, empowerment and labour market dimensions.
SC: GDI is the HDI by sex, so it needs earned income by sex.

Q: The Gender Inequality Index (GII) is based on which three dimensions?
A) Reproductive health, empowerment and economic status (labour market)
B) Health, education and income
C) Literacy, IMR and life expectancy
D) Undernourishment, wasting and stunting
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 13
EXP: **GII**: **reproductive health** (MMR, adolescent birth rate), **empowerment** (parliamentary seats held by women, secondary education of those aged 25+) and **economic status** (female LFPR).
SC: GII = Reproductive health + Empowerment + Labour.

Q: Which of the following is NOT an indicator of the Global Hunger Index?
A) Maternal mortality ratio
B) Undernourishment
C) Child stunting
D) Under-five mortality rate
ANS: A
T: Odd-one-out
D: Medium
P: 14
EXP: GHI uses four indicators: **undernourishment**, **child wasting** (low weight-for-height), **child stunting** (low height-for-age) and **child mortality** (under-5). **MMR** belongs to the GII.
SC: GHI = Undernourishment + Wasting + Stunting + Under-5 mortality.

Q: Child wasting, used in the Global Hunger Index, reflects:
A) acute undernutrition (low weight for height)
B) chronic undernutrition (low height for age)
C) obesity
D) low birth weight
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 14
EXP: **Wasting = low weight-for-height = acute** undernutrition. **Stunting = low height-for-age = chronic** undernutrition.
SC: Wasting is acute (weight), stunting is chronic (height).

Q: The Physical Quality of Life Index (PQLI) is based on:
A) literacy rate, infant mortality rate and life expectancy at age one
B) GNI per capita, schooling and life expectancy at birth
C) poverty, unemployment and inflation
D) internet affordability and e-government
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 14
EXP: **PQLI** (Morris D. Morris) = **literacy rate + IMR + life expectancy at age one**. It deliberately excludes income.
SC: PQLI = Literacy + IMR + LE at age 1, with no income.

Q: The Human Poverty Index considers deprivations in:
A) longevity, knowledge and standard of living
B) income only
C) housing and sanitation only
D) gender parity only
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 14
EXP: The **HPI** measured deprivation in **longevity, knowledge and a decent standard of living**, the same dimensions as the HDI. It was later replaced by the **Multidimensional Poverty Index**.
SC: HPI = deprivation in the HDI dimensions.

Q: The "Digital Quality of Life Index", on which India was ranked 57/85 as per the notes, is published by:
A) Surfshark
B) UNDP
C) World Bank
D) ITU
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 14
EXP: **Surfshark's** Digital Quality of Life Index covers **internet affordability, internet quality, electronic infrastructure, electronic security and e-government**. India was ranked **57/85** per the notes.
SC: DQL is a private index (Surfshark).

Q: The SDG India Index is prepared by:
A) NITI Aayog
B) NSO
C) UNDP India
D) RBI
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 14
EXP: The **SDG India Index** (first released 2018) ranks States and UTs on SDG progress. It is prepared by **NITI Aayog**. MoSPI maintains the **NIF** and the SDG progress reports.
SC: NITI publishes the SDG ranking index. MoSPI handles the SDG indicators.

@topic Surveys & Censuses
Q: The Urban Frame Survey (UFS) is conducted by FOD, NSO:
A) quinquennially, to update UFS blocks as the frame for selecting FSUs in urban areas

D) monthly, for urban prices
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: The **UFS** forms and updates **urban blocks**, which serve as the **urban FSU frame** for NSO surveys. It is done **quinquennially** by **FOD**.

Q: The Quarterly Employment Survey (QES) of the Ministry of Labour and Employment covers how many sectors?

B) 8
C) 12
D) 5
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 16
EXP: **QES** covers **9 sectors**: manufacturing, construction, trade, transport, education, health, accommodation & restaurants, IT/BPO, and financial services.
SC: QES covers 9 sectors, the organised ones with 10+ workers.

Q: The Annual Survey of Unincorporated Sector Enterprises (ASUSE) is devoted to:

B) registered factories

D) government enterprises

D: Easy

@topic Core Statistics

P: 18

SC: Eight groups: Income, Production, Budget, Money, Capital market, Indices, External, Demography.

D: Hard
P: 18

Q: Under the core statistics list, the Balance of Payments and Foreign Exchange Reserves are released by the RBI with periodicity respectively:
