EXP: The **IS Wing, DQAD (Kolkata)** plans, designs, processes and publishes the **ASI**. **FOD** does central-sample field work, and the **Labour Bureau** processes **Part-II**.
SC: ASI is run from Kolkata by the IS Wing.

Q: The ASI covers which of the following?

B) 1, 2 and 4 only

It also covers activities of **manufacturing, repair services, electricity generation, transmission and distribution, gas, water supply and cold storage**. **Unregistered** (informal) units belong to the **FuS/ASUSE**, not the ASI.

Q: ASI is conducted under:

Q: Under the ASI sample scheme, each stratum is formed on the basis of:
A) State × District × Sector × 3-digit NIC-2008
B) State × 4-digit NIC-2008
C) District × employment size class
D) State × Sector × 2-digit NIC
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 2
EXP: **Sample-scheme strata = State × District × Sector × 3-digit NIC-2008**. Units are arranged in **descending order of employees** and drawn by **circular systematic sampling**, with an **even number (minimum 4)** per stratum split into **4 sub-samples**.
SC: Strata are State-District-Sector-NIC3, with at least 4 units in 4 sub-samples.

Q: In the ASI sampling design, which sub-samples are given to NSO (FOD) and which to the State/UT for data collection?
A) Sub-samples 1 and 3 to NSO (FOD); 2 and 4 to State/UT
B) Sub-samples 1 and 2 to NSO (FOD); 3 and 4 to State/UT
C) Sub-samples 2 and 4 to NSO (FOD); 1 and 3 to State/UT
D) All four to NSO (FOD)
ANS: A
D: Hard
P: 2
EXP: **Sub-samples 1 and 3 go to FOD** and **2 and 4 to the State/UT**. The **Central Sample** = census units + sub-samples 1 and 3. The **State Sample** = census units + sub-samples 2 and 4. **Pooled estimates** use census units + all 4 sub-samples.
SC: Odd sub-samples go to the Centre, even ones to the State.

Q: Under the ASI design, the State Sample consists of:
A) all census units plus the units in the two sub-samples given to the State/UT
B) only the two sub-samples given to the State/UT
C) all census units only
D) all four sub-samples, excluding census units
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 3
EXP: **State Sample = census units (collected by FOD, processed by the IS Wing) + State sub-samples (2 and 4)**. This lets States derive **State-level estimates**. **Pooling** both samples increases the sample size.
SC: Every sample includes the census units; only the sub-samples differ.

@topic IIP & Eight Core Industries
Q: The formula used for compiling the Index of Industrial Production (IIP) is:
A) Laspeyres' formula
B) Paasche's formula
C) Fisher's ideal formula
D) Marshall-Edgeworth formula
ANS: A
D: Easy
P: 5
EXP: **IIP = Laspeyres' (base-weighted) quantity index**, with **base 2011-12**, released by **ESD, NSO**. For the all-India IIP, **value figures** of output are **deflated by the WPI**.
SC: IIP is a Laspeyres index of volume.

Q: The IIP is meant to measure changes over time in:
A) the volume of output of the industrial sector, without reflecting price changes
B) the value of industrial output at current prices
C) the prices of industrial goods
D) the employment in industry
ANS: A
T: Conceptual
D: Easy
P: 5
EXP: IIP measures **volume**, not value. **Price changes are removed**, and where only values are available they are **deflated by the WPI**.
SC: IIP measures volume, so prices are removed.

Q: As per the notes, the quick estimates of IIP are released on the 12th of every month with a lag of:
A) six weeks
B) two weeks
C) four months
D) one quarter
ANS: A
D: Medium
P: 5
EXP: As per the notes, the IIP **quick estimates** come out on the **12th** (the previous working day if the 12th is a holiday) with a **6-week lag**. IIP draws on data from **14 source agencies**.
SC: Per the notes: the 12th of the month, a 6-week lag, 14 sources.
TIP: Always check the latest MoSPI release calendar. Release dates and lags for IIP have been revised in recent years.

Q: The IIP is compiled from secondary data supplied to ESD, NSO by how many source agencies?

D) 5

D: Medium
P: 5
EXP: **14 source agencies** in various ministries and departments supply IIP data. The largest is **DPIIT** for manufacturing; mining data come from the **IBM** and electricity data from the **CEA**.
SC: IIP has 14 sources, led by DPIIT.

Q: Which of the following is NOT a reason for the divergence between growth rates of manufacturing based on IIP and ASI?
A) IIP covers unregistered units while ASI covers only registered units
B) IIP is based on a fixed set of items and factories from the base period, whereas the ASI frame changes
C) IIP growth is based on volume of production while ASI growth is based on value added
D) IIP establishments are generally larger in size, whereas ASI covers both large and small units
ANS: A
T: Odd-one-out
D: Hard
P: 6
EXP: The notes' reasons are: (i) **IIP has a fixed item and factory basket** while ASI captures **new items and factories**; (ii) IIP uses a **smaller sample**; (iii) **IIP measures volume** while **ASI measures value added**; (iv) **IIP units are larger**, and smaller units grow faster from a low base, so IIP growth is lower. IIP does **not** cover unregistered units.
SC: Fixed basket, smaller sample, volume vs value, bigger units.

D: Hard
P: 6
EXP: The notes state explicitly that **IIP growth rates are comparatively lower** because **IIP units are larger** while **smaller units grow faster** from a low base. R explains A.
SC: Big units grow slowly, so IIP growth is lower.
