## UN FPOS
! **10 principles**. Adopted by UNSC **1994** → ECOSOC **2013** → **UNGA 68/261 (29 Jan 2014)** → India's Cabinet **2016**. **World Statistics Day** comes from **64/267** (first held **20.10.2010**).
- Key phrases: "practical utility, impartial, equal access" = **P1**; "entitled to comment on misuse" = **P4**; "quality, timeliness, cost, burden" = **P5**; "natural or legal persons … strictly confidential" = **P6**. P7-P10: **Law, Coordination, Standards, Cooperation**.
## System & legal base
! Chain: **1949** CSU → **1950** NSS → **1951** CSO → **1967** Computer Centre → **1970** NSSO → **1999** MoSPI → **2019** NSO.
! Constitution: **Census = Union 69**; statistics = **Union 94 + Concurrent 45**; vital statistics = **Concurrent 30**. Article **246** distributes the Lists.
- Acts in date order: Payment of Wages **1936** → Census and Factories **1948** → RBD **1969** → CoS **2008**.
- Central offices: **ORGI (MHA)**, since 1960 also for vital statistics; **DGCI&S** trade; **DES (MoA&FW)** agriculture; **Labour Bureau** (1946) CPI-IW/AL/RL.
- Census 2011: 15th census; **35 → 30 → 9** schedules (houselisting, population, NPR); **640 districts** (2001: 593).
- SRS: baseline → continuous → half-yearly → match → verify → verbal autopsy. MRS (**1965**) came before the RBD Act.
## MoSPI & NSO
- MoSPI = Statistics + Programme Implementation (**1999**). The CSI is Secretary MoSPI, Chief Statistician and Secretary NSC.
- CSO divisions: **NAD, ESD, SSD, CPD, Training** (+ Computer Centre). NSSO divisions: **SDRD, FOD, DPD, CPD**.
- The Population Census is **RGI/MHA**, while the Economic Census is **MoSPI**. This pair is a classic trap.
## NSC & identities
! Rangarajan report **2001** → NSC resolution **2005** → constituted **2006**. 1 Chair + 4 Members + 1 ex officio; the CSI is Secretary.
! **GDP(mp) = GVA(bp) + product taxes − product subsidies**. National Income = **NNI at factor cost (N-N-F)**. **GNDI** adds current transfers. **Investment = domestic saving + foreign saving**. E&O = the balancing item.
! SDG ladder **17 → 169 → 232 → 306** (goals, targets, global indicators, India's NIF). The SDG dashboard was launched on **29 June 2019** with the UNRC.
- Quarterly GDP comes at quarter end **+ 2 months**. The PE of the year comes out with Q4 at end-May.
