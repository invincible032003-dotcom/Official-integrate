## NSSO & NSS
! NSS was a **Directorate in 1950** and became the **NSSO in 1970**, with a non-official chair on its governing council. Its journal is **Sarvekshana**.
- Survey life-cycle: **SDRD** (design) → **FOD** (field, Delhi/Faridabad; book: **6-49-116**) → **DPD** (processing, Kolkata) → **CPD** (publication).
! Decennial programme: **employment and consumption every 5 years**; land, debt and similar stocks every 10 years; **3 of the 10 years are open** for other subjects.
- 70th (2013) and 77th (2019) rounds covered land, SAS and debt. **76th** = disability + WaSH. **73rd** = unincorporated enterprises.
- Census literacy uses age **7+**; international adult literacy uses **15+**. 2011 literacy: **74 / 82 (male) / 65 (female)**.
## PI Wing, ISI, health
! **TPP: 20 points, 66 items**. **IPMD threshold ₹150 crore**. **MPLADS: ₹5 crore** per MP per year, for durable community assets.
- **ISI** founded **1931**, ISI Act **1959**. Statistics Day **29 June**; World Statistics Day **20 October**.
- **NFHS and DLHS** run by **IIPS**; the **AHS** by the **RGI** (8 EAG States + Assam).
! **HDI**: 3 dimensions and 4 indicators (LE, MYS, EYS, GNI per capita), **geometric mean**. Tiers split at **0.80 / 0.70 / 0.55**. IMR is a **PQLI** indicator, not an HDI one.
## Rangarajan Commission (agriculture & industry)
- Crop area: **TRS** (land-record States), **EARAS** (non-land-record States), remote sensing (NE). Give TRS "**national importance**". Use **small area estimation** below district level. ICS supplies a **correction factor**.
- Livestock and agriculture censuses: a **20% sample**, integrated. The Agricultural Census Commissioner should be an **Additional Secretary**-rank statistician.
- ASI: directories of units with **10+ workers**. Unregistered units: **OAME (0 hired) / NDME (1-5) / DME (6+)**. **Publish standard errors**.
- IIP: revise the base **every 5 years**. State IIPs should use **GVA** weights. Interim response-rate target: **60%** of production in the first month and **80%** at the final revision.
## MSP
! **CACP** recommends MSP. The 2018-19 Budget set MSP at **1.5 × A2+FL** cost; C2 is the reference cost. **PM-AASHA** = **PSS + PDPS + PPSS**. **GrAMs**: 22,000 rural haats.
