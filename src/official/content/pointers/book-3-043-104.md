## Education indicators
! **GER** counts all ages; **NER** only the right age group. **GER − NER** = over- and under-age enrolment.
! Promotion + repetition + dropout = **100%**. **Coefficient of efficiency = ideal pupil-years ÷ actual pupil-years** (e.g. 6 ÷ 7.14 ≈ 0.84). YIPG = pupil-years per graduate.
- Transition means crossing a level (primary → upper primary). Cohort indicators are affected by **migration**.
## PLFS 2019-20 (book)
! LFPR **40.1**, WPR **38.2**, UR **4.8** (all ages). At age 15+: LFPR **53.5**, WPR **50.9**. Educated UR **10.1**, youth UR **15.0**. Urban female youth UR is the highest.
- Rural women are mostly **self-employed** and about **75% work in agriculture**. Urban women are mostly **salaried**.
- About **70%** of non-farm workers are in proprietary or partnership units. **67-52-54%** of salaried workers have no written contract, no paid leave and no social security respectively.
## PLFS design & concepts
! Launched **Apr 2017**; first annual report **May 2019**. Fieldwork **suspended 18 Mar 2020**, resumed **1 Jun 2020**, revisits by **phone**.
! Urban **1 + 3 visits**; rural 1 visit, **25% per quarter**. **Nagaland** has a difficult-villages stratum. **12,800 FSUs allotted** (12,569 surveyed). **8 households per FSU** (SRSWOR). Second-stage strata: rural **3**, urban **4**. Schedule **10.4**. Transgender persons were counted with males.
- Household: a **6-month** rule for visitors and absentees. A paying guest is in; a tenant is not. **Living together** outweighs a common kitchen. Household types: rural **6**, urban **4** (365-day income).
- Literacy means read and write with understanding. Education years are counted from class I. Technical education has **16** categories.
! Activity codes: 11-51 employed · 81-82 unemployed · **91-99 not in the labour force**. **92** = domestic duties only; **93** = domestic duties plus collection of goods. Current-only codes: **42, 61, 62, 71, 72, 82, 98**.
! **CWS**: 1 hour on any day. **CDS**: at most 2 activities a day. Industry coded at **NIC 5-digit**, occupation at **NCO 3-digit**.
- Hours actually worked: **18th ICLS**. Waiting time counts; commuting does not. Self-employed earnings = output − costs (**basic price**). Earnings of helpers = 0.
- Enterprise types: proprietary and partnership = **informal sector**. Public company needs **≥ 7** members; private company **≤ 200**. Railways and Posts are Government departments. Social security benefits combine **PF/pension, gratuity, health** (7 combinations).
! **15th ICLS** = informal **sector** (units); **17th ICLS** = informal **employment** (jobs). The **Delhi Group** deals with the informal sector.
- **PU** = unemployed ÷ **population**; UR = PU ÷ (WPR + PU). NIC sections **A-U**; NCO divisions **1-9** in skill order (X0 = new job-seekers).
