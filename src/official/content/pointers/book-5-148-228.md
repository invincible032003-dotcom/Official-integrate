## Legal, coverage, design
! Old ASI: Act **1953** / Rules **1959**. From **2010-11**: **CoS Act 2008 / Rules 2011**. Part I goes to the **IS Wing (Kolkata)**, Part II to the **Labour Bureau**. Reference = financial year; fieldwork **Nov-Jul**.
! Covers registered factories, bidi (Beedi Workers Act **1966**) and utilities **not registered with the CEA**. Excludes defence and departmental workshops. Uses the **establishment** approach. A **joint return** needs the same State, sector and **3-digit NIC**.
- The **Nodal Officer** must be of **Joint Secretary** rank or above. The Statistics Officer runs the survey. Prosecution of officials is under **Section 25**.
! Design from **2015-16** (Manna sub-group). Census scheme: NE States, Ladakh, A&N. Employment thresholds **50 / 75 / 100** (city-UTs / six mid States / others). **Salt (0893)** is always in the census scheme. Sample: **State × District × Sector × NIC-3**, 4 sub-samples, **odd sub-samples Central, even State**.
- NIC **1970 / 1998 / 2004 / 2008** map to ISIC Rev **2 / 3 / 3.1 / 4**. **NPCMS-2011** is based on **CPC 2.0** and has 7 digits: **5 sections, 39 divisions, 192 groups, 786 classes, 1503 sub-classes**.
## Concepts
! Factory = **10+ workers with power / 20+ without** (Rajasthan and Maharashtra doubled these). Muscle power does not count as power.
- **Software** is the only intangible counted in fixed capital. Invested capital uses **physical** working capital; productive capital uses **total** working capital. Loans = **long-term only**.
! **CE = wages (emoluments) + supplements**. Output → **GVA** (− input) → **NVA** (− depreciation) → net income (− rent and interest) → **profit** (− CE).
- Ex-factory value = net of taxes + subsidies = **basic price**. R&D is added to both output and input, so it has **no effect on GVA**.
## Blocks
! There are **14 blocks (A-N)**. **DSL** changes yearly; **PSL** is permanent. Status codes: **1-3 surveyed**, **4 = deleted/zero case**, **5/7/8 = casualties**. After 3 years dormant (code 3), the unit is deleted (code 4).
- Block B: **51%** Government share = Government unit. **LLP** is a hybrid of company and partnership. **CIN** exists only for companies (codes 4-7). **ISO 14000** series. Formal training = **200 hours**.
- Block C: fully depreciated assets are kept at **Re 1**. A **financial lease** is recorded by the lessee; an **operating lease** by the lessor. SLM = (cost − scrap) ÷ life; **book** depreciation is used, not tax depreciation.
- Blocks E-J: home workers go to **F-1**; regular bonus counts as wages; welfare in kind goes in **item 12**. Interest received goes to **G-10**, interest paid to **F-10**. A **production subsidy** goes to **G-12**; a **product subsidy** to **Block J**. Broadband cut-off **256 kbit/s**.
- Part II measures absenteeism and turnover for **regular direct workers** only (ILO **C-160**).
## Fieldwork, penalties, estimation
! Notice sent by **Registered AD**; return due within **one month**. Web portal since **2012-13**. Show-cause notice gives **30 days**.
! Penalties: refusal **₹1k / ₹5k** (company) · false statement **6 months + ₹1k / ₹5k** · destruction or obstruction **6 months + ₹2k / ₹10k** · after conviction, a **14-day** grace period, then a daily fine.
- Estimation: stratum estimate = **mean of the sub-sample estimates**; **variance with 2 sub-samples = (d²)/4**. A biased estimator is judged by **MSE**. A **void stratum** is merged with the nearest one.
- The Factories Act excludes **mines and railway running sheds**; **printing and binding** are explicitly covered.
