## Schedule form & scrutiny
! Net value = gross value − accumulated depreciation. **Land and CWIP** are kept outside the depreciable sub-total. **Working capital = current assets − current liabilities**.
- Energy units: electricity in **kWh**, coal in **tonnes**, gas in **kg**. The schedule allows **10 indigenous inputs, 5 imported inputs, 10 products**.
! Scrutiny thresholds: output/input ratio **0.5**; distributive expenses and bonus **20%**.
## Ready reference (balance sheet and P&L → ASI blocks)
- External works (land improvement) go to **C-1**; integral works to the building (**C-2**). Pollution control equipment goes to **C-6**; utilities and office equipment to **C-7**. Working animals are fixed assets.
- Trading stock goes with raw materials (**D-1**). Sundry debtors **D-9**; advances received are creditors; short-term bank credit **D-13**; term loans **D-17**.
- A welfare uniform goes to **E-12**; a work uniform to **F-3**. Job work done by others for the unit **F-1**; done by the unit for others **G-1**. Brand royalty **F-3**; mining royalty **F-9**.
- Duty on inputs goes to **Block I**, duty on outputs to **Block J**. Purchased power **H-16**; self-generated power **H-15**.
## Tabulation & codes
! **GFCF = NFCF + depreciation**. **GCF = GFCF + change in stock**. Invested capital = fixed capital + physical working capital. The factory count uses status codes **1-4**; other aggregates use **1-3**.
- NIC coverage: processing close to agriculture is covered; growing crops is not. Repair is covered; trade, finance and education are not.
- ISO: **14001** EMS (like 9001 QMS), **14020s** labels, **14040s** LCA, **14060s** GHG (**14067** product carbon footprint).
## FAQs
- Joint returns are decided at the **State** level. ASI uses the **company books** (not tax books). A lease of **30+ years** counts as ownership. Depreciation applies to tangibles, amortisation to intangibles.
- Apprentices are casual; probationers are regular. Fines are **transfers**, not production costs. Transport memo items were added from **2019-20**. Tax audit threshold in the source: **₹1 crore**.
## Accounting basics
! **DEAD CLIC**: Debits increase Expenses, Assets, Drawings. Credits increase Liabilities, Income, Capital. Journal → ledger → trial balance → final accounts.
- Account types: real (what comes in / what goes out), personal (receiver / giver), nominal (expenses / income). **Ind AS 116**: a financial lease sits with the **lessee**, matching the ASI economic-ownership test.
