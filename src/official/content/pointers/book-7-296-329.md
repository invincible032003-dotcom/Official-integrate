## Natural capital & SEEA
! "Natural capital": Schumacher, *Small is Beautiful* (**1973**). The **Dasgupta** Expert Group on green accounts was set up in **2011** and reported in **2013**.
! **SEEA-CF 2012** (a statistical standard) and **SEEA-EA 2021** (**52nd UNSC**). CF covers **flows, stocks, activity**; EA covers **extent, condition, services**.
- Energy is aggregated in **joules**. Water has three accounts: flow, asset and economic. SEEA follows the SNA **residence** principle.
## Ecosystem accounting
! Account order: **Extent → Condition → Services → Monetary asset → Thematic**. Asset value = **NPV of future service flows**. Ecosystem types follow the **IUCN** typology.
- Services: **provisioning** (goods), **regulating** (filtration, flood control), **cultural** (recreation, tourism). Valuation uses **exchange value**, excluding consumer surplus.
- **NWFP** means no wood at all; **NTFP** excludes only timber.
## Minerals & indices
- UNFC-2009 categories: A = recoverable now, B = potentially, C = not commercial. Coal zones: **200 m proved, 1 km indicated, 2 km inferred**.
! **Shannon-Wiener H' = −Σ p ln p** (a single species gives 0; equal abundance gives ln S). **Soil Nutrient Index** = (1·L + 2·M + 3·H) ÷ N, with cut-offs **1.67 / 2.33**.
## IUCN, FDES, CBD, CITES
! **IUCN (1948)** Red List has **9 categories**. Threatened = **CR, EN, VU**.
! **FDES 2013** has **6 components**: conditions, resources, residuals, extreme events, human settlements, protection. EnviStats Vol I = statistics; **Vol II = accounts**.
- **CBD** signed **5 Jun 1992**, in force **29 Dec 1993**. **CITES** signed **1973**, in force **1975**. Appendix I banned, II controlled, III at one country's request.
- Protected-area categories Ia/Ib to VI. India's protected areas: National Parks, Wildlife Sanctuaries, Conservation Reserves and Community Reserves.
## India's accounts
! **NCAVES** (EU-funded; **UNSD, UNEP, CBD**) pilot countries: **Brazil, China, India, Mexico, South Africa**. **SDG 15.9.1** covers SEEA implementation; SEEA feeds about **40 indicators in 9 goals**.
- Environment-accounts strategy (Dec 2021) for **2022-26**: energy, material flow accounts (MFA), ocean and urban accounts. The IMG on environment accounts is chaired by the **DG, CSO**. **UNCEEA** (2005), with India as a member. The **London Group** covers environmental accounting.
- Land degradation accounts compare **2005-06 and 2015-16**. The soil erosion account is physical only; carbon accounts are physical and monetary.
