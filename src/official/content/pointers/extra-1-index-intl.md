## Index number formulae & tests
! **Laspeyres** Σp1q0/Σp0q0 (base weights, upward bias) · **Paasche** Σp1q1/Σp0q1 (downward bias) · **Fisher** = √(L×P) · **Bowley** = (L+P)/2 · **Marshall-Edgeworth** uses q0+q1 · **Walsh** √(q0q1) · **Kelly** fixed q.
! Tests: **Fisher** passes **time reversal + factor reversal** but fails the circular test. **Circular test** is passed by **Kelly** and the **simple GM of relatives**. Simple aggregative Σp1/Σp0 **fails the unit test**. L and P fail both reversal tests. M-E passes time reversal only.
- Time reversal: P01 × P10 = 1. Factor reversal: P01 × Q01 = V01. Circular: P01 × P12 × P20 = 1 (time reversal extended).
- AM of relatives with **p0q0 weights = Laspeyres**; HM with **p1q1 weights = Paasche**. The family budget method = Laspeyres.
- **Base shift**: old index ÷ old value of the new base × 100. **Splice**: new index × old link value ÷ 100. **Deflate**: money ÷ index × 100. Purchasing power = 100 ÷ CPI.
- CPI (2012) uses the **geometric mean (Jevons)** at the elementary level. The GDP deflator is an implicit Paasche index. **Sensex 1978-79 = 100**; **Nifty 1995 = 1000**.
## International bodies & standards
! **UN Statistical Commission 1946** (ECOSOC functional commission, **24 members**; UNSD is its secretariat). **ISI 1885, The Hague**. **SIAP, Chiba** (ESCAP, Bangkok). **IAOS** is part of the ISI. **PARIS21** (1999) is hosted by the **OECD**.
! IMF: **SDDS 1996** (India subscribed 1996) → **GDDS 1997** → **SDDS Plus 2012** → **e-GDDS 2015**. Metadata go on the **DSBB**, data on the **NSDP**.
- **ICP / PPP** is coordinated by the **World Bank** (India: 3rd largest in PPP terms; ICP 2021 released 2024). **LSMS** is the World Bank's, **MICS** UNICEF's, **DHS** USAID's.
- ICLS: **15th (1993) informal sector**, **17th (2003) informal employment**, **18th (2008) working time**, **19th (2013) forms of work** (employment = work for pay or profit), **20th (2018) ICSE-18**.
- City groups: **Voorburg** services · **Ottawa** prices · **Delhi** informal sector · **Rio** poverty · **Canberra** household income · **Washington** disability · **London** environmental accounts · **Wiesbaden** business registers · **Oslo** energy.
! Classification pairs: **ISIC Rev.4 → NIC-2008** · **ISCO-08 → NCO-2015** · **CPC → NPCMS** · **HS (WCO, 6-digit) → ITC-HS (8-digit)** · **ISCED 2011 (UNESCO UIS, Montreal)** · **ICD-11 (from 1 Jan 2022)** · **COICOP** (household consumption) · **COFOG** (government) · **BEC** (end use).
- SNA editions: **1953, 1968, 1993, 2008, 2025**. **BPM6 (2009)** → BPM7 (2025). GSBPM, GSIM, CSPA and GAMSO come from **UNECE HLG-MOS**. **SDMX** is for aggregates, **DDI** for microdata.
- SDG architecture: **IAEG-SDGs**; indicator tiers I/II/III. First **UN World Data Forum, Cape Town 2017** (Global Action Plan).

- **CBHI**: National Health Profile · **NCRB**: Crime in India / ADSI / Prison Statistics · **MoRTH**: Road Accidents in India · **IBM**: Indian Minerals Yearbook · **FSI**: ISFR (biennial) · **CEA**: General Review · **Coal Controller**: Coal Directory · **DES**: Agricultural Statistics at a Glance · **DAHD**: Basic Animal Husbandry Statistics · **Labour Bureau**: Indian Labour Journal · **MoE**: UDISE+, AISHE (since 2010-11) · **NCERT**: NAS · **ASER** comes from **Pratham**, not the Government.
- International: **WEO** (IMF), **HDR** (UNDP), **WDR/WDI** (World Bank), **GGGI** (WEF), **GII (innovation)** (WIPO), **World Happiness Report** (SDSN), **GHI** (Welthungerhilfe + Concern Worldwide).
