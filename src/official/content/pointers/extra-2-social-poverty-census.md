## Health & education
! NFHS-5 (2019-21): **TFR 2.0**, institutional births **88.6%**, stunting **35.5** / wasting **19.3** / underweight **32.1**, anaemia in women **57%** and in children **67%**, sex ratio **1020**, SRB **929**.
! MMR (SRS): 167 (2011-13) → 130 → 113 → 103 → **97 (2018-20)** → 93 (2019-21). IMR: 32 (2018) → 30 → 28 → **27 (2021)**. SDG 3: **MMR < 70, NMR ≤ 12, U5MR ≤ 25** by 2030.
- NHP 2017 targets: LE 70 and TFR 2.1 by 2025, MMR 100 by 2020, IMR 28 by 2019, health spending 2.5% of GDP. HMIS started in **2008** (NRHM). NHA estimates come from **NHSRC** (SHA 2011).
- **RTE**: ages 6-14, PTR **30:1** (primary) and **35:1** (upper primary). **NEP 2020**: higher-education GER **50% by 2035**. AISHE GER uses ages **18-23**. **GPI** = female GER ÷ male GER.
## Gender, disability, age groups
- India's sex ratio = **females per 1,000 males**; the UN uses males per 100 females. **GDI** = female HDI ÷ male HDI. **GII** = reproductive health + empowerment + labour market (no income). **GGGI** (WEF) = economy, education, health, politics.
- Gender budgeting since **2005-06**. TUS 2019: women **~299 min/day** of unpaid domestic work vs men **~97**. Disability: **2.21%** (Census 2011). **RPwD Act 2016: 21 disabilities**. LASI is by **IIPS**. Youth = **15-29** (NYP 2014). Elderly = **60+**.
## Poverty & inequality
! Poverty lines: **Alagh 1979** (2400/2100 kcal) → **Lakdawala 1993** (State lines, CPI-AL/CPI-IW) → **Tendulkar 2009** (MRP, no calorie anchor; **37.2% → 21.9%**, ₹816/₹1000 per month) → **Rangarajan 2014** (MMRP; **29.5%**, ₹972/₹1407).
- FGT: **α = 0** headcount, **1** poverty gap, **2** severity. **Gini = 1 − 2 × area under the Lorenz curve**. **Palma** = top 10% ÷ bottom 40%.
! **Global MPI** (UNDP + OPHI, 2010, Alkire-Foster, **MPI = H × A**, poor if deprived in ≥ 1/3). **National MPI** (NITI) adds **maternal health + bank account** (12 indicators): **24.85% → 14.96%** (NFHS-4 to NFHS-5), **13.5 crore** people exited.
! **HCES 2022-23**: MPCE **₹3,773 rural / ₹6,459 urban**. **HCES 2023-24**: **₹4,122 / ₹6,996**. Urban-rural gap **84% (2011-12) → 70%**. Three questionnaires in three visits, MMRP. Food share under 50%, consistent with Engel's law.
- World Bank extreme poverty line: USD 1.90 (2011 PPP) → **USD 2.15** (2017 PPP) → **USD 3.00** (2021 PPP, 2025). HDI uses a **geometric mean** (since 2010); India is in the **medium** category (0.55-0.70). SDG India Index: Front Runner **65-99**.
## Census
! 2011: **121.09 crore**; growth **17.7%**; density **382**; sex ratio **943**; CSR **918**; literacy **74.04** (M 82.14 / F 65.46); urban **~31%**; SC **16.6%**, ST **8.6%**; **640 districts**; **7,935 towns** (4,041 statutory + 3,894 census); **53** million-plus UAs; **6.41 lakh villages**.
- Extremes: sex ratio Kerala **1084** / Haryana **879**; CSR lowest Haryana **834**; literacy Kerala ~94 / Bihar ~61.8; density Bihar **1,106** / Arunachal **17**; most populous UP, least Sikkim. WPR **39.8%** (female 25.5%).
! Census town = **5,000+** people, **75%+** male main workers in non-agriculture, **400+/sq km**. Main worker = **183+ days**. Reference moment for 2011: 00:00 on **1 March 2011**.
! **Census 2027** (the 16th): reference **1 March 2027** (snow-bound areas **1 October 2026**). It is **digital** (mobile apps, self-enumeration), includes **caste** enumeration, and runs under the **Census Act 1948**.
- TFR = 5 × Σ ASFR. GRR counts daughters; **NRR = 1** is replacement (TFR about 2.1). Doubling time ≈ 70 ÷ r. Projection (2020 report): about **152 crore by 2036**. India became the most populous country in **2023**.
