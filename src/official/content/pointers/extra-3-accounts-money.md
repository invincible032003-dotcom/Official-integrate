## National accounts concepts
! SNA boundary: **own-account goods** and **imputed rent** are **in**; unpaid household services are **out**; **transfers** (pensions) are **out**; second-hand sales count only the **commission**; **share purchases** are not investment. **R&D is capitalised** (SNA 2008). **FISIM** is the service value in interest margins.
! 2011-12 series: **GVA at basic prices** plus headline **GDP at market prices**; **MCA-21** data (enterprise approach); **effective labour input** method for the unorganised sector.
- Institutional sectors: **NFC, FC, GG, HH, NPISH**. Mixed income comes from the self-employed; CE = wages + employer contributions. Official per capita income = **per capita NNI**. Capital stock is estimated by the **PIM**.
- Deflator = nominal ÷ real × 100 (Paasche-type). **Double deflation** deflates output and inputs separately. I-O: **a_ij = x_ij ÷ X_j**, **X = (I − A)⁻¹F**, and **Hawkins-Simon** gives viability.
- **GSDP** is compiled by the **State DES**. Satellite accounts include the Tourism Satellite Account and SEEA. Naoroji (1867-68) and V.K.R.V. Rao (1931-32) made early estimates; the NIC (1949) made the first official ones.
## Money & banking
! **M0** = currency in circulation + bankers' deposits with the RBI + other deposits with the RBI. **M1** = currency with the public + demand deposits + other deposits with the RBI. **M2** = M1 + post office savings. **M3** = M1 + time deposits (broad money). **M4** = M3 + all post office deposits (excluding NSC).
- New aggregates **NM1-NM3** and liquidity aggregates **L1-L3**: **Y.V. Reddy Working Group (1998)**. Money multiplier = M3 ÷ M0. **BSR** (RBI) covers credit and deposits. FI-Index weights: **Access 35 / Usage 45 / Quality 20**.
## Fiscal & external
! **FD** = total expenditure − (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts). **RD** = revenue expenditure − revenue receipts. **PD** = FD − interest. **ERD** = RD − grants for capital assets. **FRBM Act 2003**; N.K. Singh review 2017. Monthly accounts come from the **CGA**.
! **BoP**: compiled by the **RBI**, quarterly, on **BPM6**. Current account = goods + services + primary income + secondary income. **Invisibles** = services + income + transfers. Terms of trade = export UVI ÷ import UVI × 100. A **REER rise means appreciation**.
- FDI Factsheet: **DPIIT**. External debt status report: **DEA**. Openness = (X + M) ÷ GDP. Import cover = reserves ÷ monthly imports.
## Energy, environment & sectors
- **1 toe = 41.868 GJ**; **1 kWh = 3.6 MJ**. **Coal** is the largest primary energy source. The energy balance tracks supply → transformation → final use. Power data come from the **CEA**, renewables from **MNRE**, oil from **PPAC**.
- **ISFR 2021**: forest cover **~21.7%**, forest + tree cover **~24.6%**; policy target **33%**. NHB **RESIDEX (2007)**; the RBI also publishes an HPI.
! Agriculture Census 2015-16: about **14.6 crore holdings**, average **1.08 ha**, marginal + small **~86%**, women operators **~14%**. Size classes: <1, 1-2, 2-4, 4-10, ≥10 ha. 20th Livestock Census: **~536 million** livestock, poultry **~852 million**. CACP covers **22 MSP crops** plus the FRP for sugarcane.
- MSME limits (2020): **1/5, 10/50, 50/250 crore** (investment / turnover), revised upward in 2025. **Udyam** since July 2020; MSMED Act 2006. Cropping intensity = GCA ÷ NSA × 100.
