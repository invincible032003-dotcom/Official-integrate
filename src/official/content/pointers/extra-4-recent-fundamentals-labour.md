## Recent results (2023-26)
! PLFS (15+, usual status): **UR 6.1% (2017-18) → 3.2% (2023-24)**; **LFPR ~60%**, **WPR ~58%** in 2023-24; female LFPR **~23% → ~42%**, led by rural self-employment. The **2025 revamp** added **monthly (CWS)** and **rural + urban quarterly** estimates.
- New bases: **GDP 2022-23**, **CPI 2024** (HCES 2023-24 weights), **IIP/WPI 2022-23**. The **2025 SNA** and **BPM7** were adopted in 2025. TUS rounds: 2019 and **2024**. **NFHS-6** fieldwork: 2023-24.
- Platforms: **eSankhyiki** (MoSPI, 29 June 2024), **NDAP** (NITI), **data.gov.in** (NIC, **NDSAP 2012**). **DGQI** comes from **DMEO**. Aspirational Districts: **112** (Health 30, Education 30, Agriculture 20, Finance/Skills 10, Infrastructure 10); Aspirational Blocks: **~500**.
- Statistics Day themes: 2022 Data for Sustainable Development; 2024 **use of data for decision-making**. WSD themes: 2015 "Better data, better lives"; 2020 "Connecting the world with data we can trust".
## Fundamentals
! Quality dimensions: **relevance, accuracy, timeliness/punctuality, accessibility/clarity, coherence/comparability**. IMF DQAF: integrity, methodological soundness, accuracy and reliability, serviceability, accessibility (plus prerequisites).
! **Sampling error** falls as n rises. **Non-sampling errors** (coverage, non-response, response, processing) occur in censuses too. A **PES** measures census coverage error. **IPNS** gives variance and exposes investigator bias. NSS **substitutes** casualty households.
- Methods: registration (CRS), census, sample survey, administrative data (cheap and frequent, but built on administrative concepts), remote sensing, big data (GST, UPI, e-way bills). Reference period vs survey period.
- Transparency: an **advance release calendar**, published methods and revision policy, **no selective pre-release**, and **free anonymised microdata**. Metadata = data about data (FPOS Principle 3).
## Labour concepts
! ILO unemployed = **no work + available + seeking**; employed = **1 hour** of work. **Potential labour force** = seeking but unavailable, or available but not seeking. **LU1** unemployment → **LU2** + time-related underemployment → **LU3** + potential LF → **LU4** composite.
! **UR(CDS) > UR(CWS) > UR(US)**. Usual status = chronic unemployment. Types: disguised (zero marginal product), seasonal, frictional, structural, cyclical.
- SDG **8.5.2** unemployment, **8.3.1** informality, **8.6.1** NEET. Workers by status: self-employed **~58%**, regular **~22%**, casual **~20%**; agriculture **~46%** of workers.
- **e-Shram** (August 2021) registers unorganised workers. **Four labour codes** replace **29 laws**. **Gig workers**: 77 lakh (2020-21) → ~2.35 crore (2029-30) per NITI. Child labour: under 14 banned (2016 amendment); **1.01 crore** child workers in 2011.
- Employment elasticity = job growth ÷ GDP growth. EPFO covers establishments with **20+** employees. QES is demand-side; PLFS is supply-side.
