# Full forms · near-miss traps
@source Full-form list + book & notes
@group fullforms
## Same letters, different meaning
! **CSO** = Central **Statistics Office** (in the list; earlier Central Statistical Organisation). **NSSO** = National Sample Survey **Office** (earlier Organisation). **NSO** = National Statistical **Office** (2019).
! **DGCI&S** = Directorate General of **Commercial Intelligence** & Statistics (not "Customs"). **DGFT** = Foreign **Trade** (not taxation).
! **O/o CAG** = **Comptroller** & Auditor General (not "Controller"). **CACP** = Agricultural **Costs** & Prices (not credit).
- **NITI** = National **Institution** (not Institute) for Transforming India. **MNCFC** = Mahalanobis National **Crop Forecasting** Centre. **NSSTA** = National Statistical **Systems** Training Academy.
- **DGFASLI** = Directorate General **Factory Advice Service** & Labour Institutes. **DGMS** = Mines **Safety**. **IBM** = Indian **Bureau** of Mines (Nagpur).
- **CEC** in the list = Central Excise **Collectorate**. **NCDC** in the list = National Centre for **Disease Control**. **NIC** in statistics = National **Industrial Classification** (not the Informatics Centre).
- **DONER** = **Development** of North Eastern Region. **DIPP** → now **DPIIT** (Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade, 2019).
## Grades (Group A ladder)
! **JTS → STS → JAG (NFSG) → SAG → HAG → HAG Plus**. JTS = Junior **Time Scale**; NFSG = **Non-Functional** Selection Grade.
## MoSPI & NSO acronyms
! **DQAD** Data Quality **Assurance** Division · **DIID** Data **Informatics and Innovation** Division · **PIMD** **Policy** Implementation & Monitoring Division · **IPMD** **Infrastructure** & Project Monitoring Division · **SDRD** Survey Design & **Research** Division.
- **e-SIGMA** = e-Survey Instrument and Generalised Multimodal Application. **OCMS** = Online Computerized Monitoring System. **NIF** = National **Indicator** Framework.
## Surveys & concepts
! **MMRP** = **Modified Mixed** Reference Period · **URP** = **Uniform** Reference Period · **CWS/CDS** = Current Weekly/Daily **Status**.
- **ASUSE** = Annual Survey of **Unincorporated Sector** Enterprises · **AQEES** = All-India Quarterly **Establishment-based** Employment Survey · **AFES** = Area Frame Establishment Survey · **WCFIES** = Working Class Family **Income and Expenditure** Survey.
- **FASAL** = Forecasting Agricultural output using **Space, Agro-meteorology and Land** based observations. **EARAS** = Establishment of an **Agency** for Reporting Agricultural Statistics.
## International
! **SDDS** = Special Data **Dissemination Standard** · **DSBB** = Dissemination Standards **Bulletin Board** · **NSDP** = National **Summary Data Page**.
- **SEEA** = System of **Environmental-Economic** Accounting · **NCAVES** = **Natural** Capital Accounting and Valuation of **Ecosystem** Services · **FDES** = Framework for the **Development** of Environment Statistics.
- **COICOP** = Classification of Individual Consumption **According to Purpose** · **COFOG** = Classification of the **Functions** of Government · **PARIS21** = Partnership in Statistics for Development in the 21st Century.
- **ICLS** = International **Conference** of Labour Statisticians · **IMTS** = International **Merchandise** Trade Statistics · **SDMX** = Statistical Data and Metadata eXchange.
## Decoding technique
- Map the letters one to one. Reject any option that adds or drops a word, even if it sounds official.
- Watch the swap pairs: Office/Organisation, Institution/Institute, Comptroller/Controller, Commercial/Customs, Assurance/Assessment, Uniform/Unified.
