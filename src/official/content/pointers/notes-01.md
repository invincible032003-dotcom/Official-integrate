## Structure & history
! **MoSPI formed 15 Oct 1999** = Department of Statistics + Department of Programme Implementation. It has two wings: **Statistics (NSO)** and **Programme Implementation** (IPMD, MPLADS, TPP).
! Chain of years: **1949** Statistical Adviser, CSU and National Income Committee → **1950** NSS → **1951** CSO → **1967** Computer Centre → **1970** NSSO → **1999** MoSPI → **2019** NSO (CSO + NSSO merged).
- National Income Committee **1949**: P.C. Mahalanobis (chair), D.R. Gadgil, V.K.R.V. Rao.
- Computer Centre hardware: **Honeywell-400 (1967) → Burroughs-3845 (1981) → DPS 7000/240 (1992)**.
- Sukhatme = agricultural statistics (CCE, IASRI). Mahalanobis = NSS and the overall system (ISI 1931; ISI Act **1959**).
## Legal & constitutional base
! Statistics = **Union List Entry 94** + **Concurrent List Entry 45**. There is no State List entry. Census = **Union 69**; vital statistics = **Concurrent 30**; economic & social planning = **Concurrent 20**.
! **CoS Act 2008** (Act 7 of 2009): enacted 7 Jan 2009, in force **11 Jun 2010**, Rules **16 May 2011**. It repealed the **CoS Act 1953**. J&K had its own **1961** Act until the **2017 amendment**.
- Other laws: Census Act **1948**, Factories Act **1948**, RBD Act **1969**, Payment of Wages **1936**.
- The NSSO Governing Council was absorbed into the **NSC**.
## ISS & training
! **ISS constituted 1 Nov 1961**; first exam **1967**; first batch **1968**. It began bottom-heavy (few senior posts).
- ISS and IES share one UPSC examination.
- **NSSTA** (earlier NASA) set up **13 Feb 2009**, **Greater Noida**. An old training unit functioned at Pushpa Bhawan, Delhi.
## NSS design & ICT
- NSS uses multi-stage, stratified PPS with **circular systematic sampling** and **interpenetrating network of sub-samples (IPNS)**.
! **e-SIGMA** (DQAD): paper → **CAPI**, multimodal **CAPI/CATI/CAWI**. It integrates **SDRD, DQAD, FOD and SCD**.
## Misc
- India follows the IMF's **SDDS** (not GDDS). The Advance Release Calendar is posted on the **DSBB**.
- **NMDS** (National Metadata Structure) sets a minimum metadata standard for comparability.
- First **COCSSO** was held in **Sept 1971**. The **Delhi Group** (1997) deals with the informal sector.
