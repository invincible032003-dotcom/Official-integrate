## Mandate & survey divisions
! NSO = nodal agency for the planned development of the statistical system. It coordinates **horizontally** (ministries) and **vertically** (States), and sets standards and core statistics. **WPI is not NSO's** (OEA, DPIIT).
! Survey divisions: **SDRD** (design and reports, **Kolkata**); **FOD** (field, **Delhi/Faridabad**, notes: **6 ZO / 53 RO / 116 SRO**; the book gives 49 ROs); **DQAD** (processing, **Kolkata**, 6 DPCs + IS Wing); **SCD** (coordination, **Delhi**).
- **Sarvekshana**: bi-annual journal, first issue **July 1977**, published by SCD.
- DQAD DPCs: **Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, Giridih, Nagpur** (Chennai is not on the list).
## Chief Statistician of India
! **Pronab Sen** (first CSI, **21 Feb 2007** – 30 Jun 2010) → **T.C.A. Anant** (1 Jul 2010 – 31 Jan 2017) → **Pravin Srivastava** (to 31 Aug 2020) → **G.P. Samanta**.
- The CSI is also **Secretary, MoSPI** and **Secretary, NSC**.
## Statistics days
! **Statistics Day 29 June** (Mahalanobis's birthday), first held **2007**. Themes: 2019 SDGs; 2020 **SDG 3 + SDG 5**; 2021 **SDG 2**; 2022 Data for Sustainable Development.
! **World Statistics Day 20 Oct**, every 5 years: **2010, 2015, 2020** (UNGA **64/267**).
## Divisions (12)
- **NAD**: GDP, SUT, IOTT, and payroll data (EPFO/ESIC/NPS). **PSD**: CPI (R/U/C) and ICP/PPP. **ESD**: IIP, EC, energy, NIC/NPCMS. **SSD**: SDGs, social statistics, environment.
- **CPD**: nodal for the CoS Act; runs **COCSSO** and **Statistics Day**; SSS scheme. **DIID**: IT and innovation. **PIMD**: created **18 Jan 2021** for data governance.
- Divisions certified under NQAF: **NAD, PSD, SDRD**.
## Price & production indices
! Base years: **CPI-AL/RL 1986-87**; **CPI (R/U/C) 2012**; **CPI-IW 2016**; **WPI and IIP 2011-12**.
! Release days: **CPI and IIP on the 12th**, **WPI on the 14th**, **CPI-IW at month-end**.
- IIP weights: **Manufacturing 77.63 / Mining 14.37 / Electricity 7.99**. Eight core industries = **40.27%** of IIP (released by OEA).
- CPI-AL covers rural wages and rural poverty lines. CPI-IW covers DA and urban poverty lines.
## Development indices
- **GHI** = undernourishment + wasting (acute) + stunting (chronic) + under-5 mortality. **PQLI** = literacy + IMR + life expectancy at age 1 (no income).
- **GII** = reproductive health + empowerment + labour market. **GDI** = HDI by sex. **Digital Quality of Life Index** = Surfshark (India 57/85). **SDG India Index** = NITI Aayog.
## Surveys & core statistics
- **UFS** (FOD, every 5 years), **QES** (MoLE, 9 sectors, 10+ workers), **ASUSE** (unincorporated enterprises), **EC** (ESD, all non-farm establishments).
- Censuses: Population **ORGI, MHA (10 yr)**; Economic **ESD**; Livestock **DAHD (5 yr)**; Agriculture **DoA&FW (5 yr)**.
- RBI surveys: **OBICUS and Industrial Outlook (quarterly)**, CCS, IESH, SPF, Bank Lending, Services & Infrastructure Outlook.
! **8 groups of core statistics**: National Income; Production; Budget; Money & Banking; Capital Market; Indices; External; Demography, Social & Environment.
- In the core list: IOTT every **5 yr** (NAD); primary market (**SEBI**); forex monthly and BoP annually (**RBI**); budget deficits (**MoF, annual**); informal sector every **5 yr** (NSO + States).
