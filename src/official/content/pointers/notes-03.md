## Setting up
! Rangarajan Commission set up **Jan 2000**, report **Aug 2001**. NSC resolution **1 Jun 2005**; NSC constituted **12 Jul 2006**.
! Composition: **part-time Chairperson + 4 part-time Members**, **CEO NITI Aayog ex officio**. The **CSI is Secretary** (not a member).
! Chairpersons in order: **Suresh Tendulkar** (first) → R. Radhakrishna → **Pronab Sen** (also first CSI) → R.B. Barman → Bimal Kumar Roy → **Rajeeva L. Karandikar** (Dec 2022).
## Mandate & powers
- **13 functions** (clauses a-m): identify **core statistics**, evolve standards, statistical audit, coordination, and advise on legislation (including an NSC statute).
- Statistical audit is about **quality**, not money. **Clause (k)** covers implementation of the standards from clauses (c)-(h).
- The NSC sets policy and standards. The **NSO is its executive wing** (per the Nov 2019 notification merging CSO and NSSO).
## Draft NSC Bill 2019 (released 10 Jan 2020)
! The Bill makes the NSC a **body corporate**. Members would include the **RBI Deputy Governor** and **CEA** ex officio, and the **CSI** as a member.
- **National Statistical Audit and Assessment Organisation** headed by a **Chief Statistical Auditor**; a **National Statistical Fund**; an independent secretariat.
- Functions: **core**, **standardisation**, **rationalisation** (avoid duplication), **advisory** (suo motu review of legislation and technology).
- Example given for the Bill's rationale: data on **homeless** persons that need to be shared.
