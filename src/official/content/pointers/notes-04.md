# Topic 4 · Field Operations Division
@source Notes 4 · pp. 1-2
@group notes
! HQ **Delhi/Faridabad**; network **6 Zonal, 53 Regional, 116 Sub-Regional Offices** (book: 49 ROs).
! **ICS**: sample check of area enumeration in about **5,000 villages** per season, and supervision of about **16,000 CCEs** per year. CCE data also give **yield rates**. A season-wise report goes to each State.
- **UFS**: phased over **5 years**; forms UFS blocks as the **urban FSU frame**. The 2017-22 phase ran on a digital platform (**NRSC**, QGIS).
- **PLFS** field work on **CAPI** (World Bank help). Quarterly urban CWS plus annual rural and urban (ps+ss, CWS).
! First **Time Use Survey**: **Jan-Dec 2019**. It measures paid and unpaid activities of men and women.
! FOD has collected **CPI (U)** prices since **May 2008**. It took over **CPI (R)** from the **Department of Posts in Sept 2018**.
- FOD collects rural retail prices for **CPI-AL/RL**, which are compiled by the **Labour Bureau, Shimla**.
- **WPI**: FOD only **facilitates** price collection for the **OEA**.
- ASI field work covers manufacturing, repair, electricity, gas, water supply and cold storage.
