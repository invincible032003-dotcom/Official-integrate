## Basics
! **1st round Oct 1950 – Mar 1951**: land utilisation, prices, wage rates, consumer expenditure.
! Round = **July-June** (agricultural year), with **4 sub-rounds of 3 months**. Some rounds lasted 6 months (e.g. the 76th).
- Design: **stratified multi-stage**, **circular systematic PPS**. **FSU** = villages / UFS blocks / sub-units. **USU** = households / enterprises.
- Strata: rural and urban per district. **Million-plus towns** are separate strata. A **special rural stratum** (all-India) holds uninhabited villages.
## Rounds 74-79
! **74th** (2016-17) Service Sector Enterprises · **75th** (2017-18) Education + Health + CES · **76th** (Jul-Dec 2018) DWSH & Housing + **Disability**.
! **77th** (Jan-Dec 2019; reference Jul 2018 – Jun 2019) **LHS + SAS** of agricultural households + **AIDIS** (+ TUS listed). Two visits: **Jan-Aug** and **Sep-Dec 2019**.
! **78th** (2020, extended to **15 Aug 2021**) **Domestic Tourism + MIS** · **79th** (Jul 2022 – Jun 2023) **CAMS + AYUSH** (first of its kind; AYUSH was first attempted in the **68th round**).
## Definitions
! Agricultural household (77th): produce value **> ₹4,000** + at least one member **self-employed in agriculture** (ps/ss, 365 days). The 70th round threshold was ₹3,000.
- Owned land includes **long-term lease / owner-like possession**. **Cooperative farms** are not household operational holdings. A kitchen garden counts as a holding.
- AIDIS: stock as on **30.06.2018**. **IOI** = % of households indebted; **AOD** = average debt; **AVA** = average assets.
- TUS: **4 AM to 4 AM** recall, **age 6+**. Per-person averages sum to **1,440 minutes**; per-participant averages do not.
- 75th Education: ages **3-35**; digital skills asked for age **5+**. The Morbidity & Health survey is **decennial**.
## Reference periods
! **365 days**: income, usual status, durables, clothing, footwear, enterprise inputs and outputs. **30 days**: consumer expenditure. **7 days**: CWS.
! **URP** = 30 days for all items. **MRP** = 365 days for clothing, bedding, footwear, education, institutional medical, durables; 30 days for the rest. **MMRP** = **7 days** for edible oil, eggs, fish, meat, vegetables, fruits, spices, beverages, processed food, pan and tobacco; 365 days for durables etc.; 30 days for the rest.
- MMRP is the most robust and is used internationally for **CPI weights**.
## Uses
- CES → poverty lines and level of living. EUS → manpower planning. NSS literacy = official estimate between censuses. Disability data → MoSJE. Debt data → RBI.
