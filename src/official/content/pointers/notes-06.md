# Topic 6 · Use of ICT in NSO
@source Notes 6 · pp. 1-3
@group notes
! **DIID** handles IT solutions and runs the proposed **Data Innovation Lab** (start-ups, academia, proof-of-concepts). It also built the **CPI Archival Web Portal**.
! **e-SIGMA** = e-Survey Instrument and Generalised Multimodal Application, built by **DQAD**. It moves surveys to **CAPI** with built-in validation, supports **CAPI + CATI + CAWI**, and links **SDRD, DQAD, FOD and SCD**.
- **7th EC (2019-20)**: geo-tagged mobile collection, real-time validation, MIS dashboards (field work through CSCs).
- **UFS 2017-22** on mobile and web apps from **NRSC Hyderabad**. Boundary polygons are drawn in **QGIS**.
! **PLFS CAPI** was developed by NSO with **World Bank** technical help.
- ICS e-schedules: **AS 2.0 = CCE sample checks** (in-house software, **2018-19**); **AS 1.0 = area enumeration** (Android app on the **GSS** platform, piloted first).
! **PIMD** created **18 Jan 2021**: data governance and **Industry 4.0**.
- **IPMD** (PI Wing) monitors Central Sector projects **above ₹150 crore** through **OCMS**.
