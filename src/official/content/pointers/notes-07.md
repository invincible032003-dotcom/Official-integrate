## System & crop area
! Decentralised: **SASA** (State) collects and **DES, MoA&FW** consolidates.
! Three groups of States: **land-record** (patwari, **girdawari**; **18 States + 5 UTs**; **86%** of area); **permanently settled** (Kerala, Odisha, WB; **EARAS**, 20% sample; **9%**); **conventional estimates** (NE hills, A&N, Lakshadweep; **5%**).
- Land use has a **nine-fold** classification, including culturable waste, current fallows and net area sown. Area sown more than once is derived, not a category.
## Production, GCES & forecasts
! Production = **area × yield**. Yield comes from **CCEs under GCES**: **stratified 3-stage**, tehsil stratum → **village** → **field** → **5 m × 5 m plot**.
- Forecasts: 1st visual (kharif) → 2nd adds inputs, pests and **remote sensing** → 3rd adds **MIU, IMD, CWWG** → 4th firm **SASA/GCES** figures → Final → "Fully Revised" in the next year.
- AE table in the notes: 1st Sep (kharif) · 2nd Feb · 3rd Apr-May · 4th Jul-Aug · Final Feb of the next year. Covers **28 major crops** (final: 28 + 3 minor). DES has since revised this calendar.
- Kharif: rice, maize, jowar, bajra, ragi, **arhar**, soybean, groundnut, cotton. Rabi: wheat, barley, **gram**, mustard, linseed.
## Censuses
! Agriculture Census: **quinquennial since 1970-71** under the FAO **WCA** (which is decennial). Unit = **operational holding**. Phases: **1 listing/ownership** → **2 detail (20% villages)** → **3 Input Survey (7% villages)**.
- 10th Agriculture Census: reference **2015-16** (Input Survey 2016-17). 11th: **2021-22**. Scheme: **ISACES**.
! Livestock Census: since **1919-20**, quinquennial. **20th in 2019**. Unit = **household**. Run by the Department of Animal Husbandry & Dairying.
- Why the two censuses can't be run together: unit, reference period, coverage and agency all differ. Suggested fix: same year, 20% sample, one agency.
## IAS scheme
! **TRS** (16 land-record States, **20%** villages, rotates over **5 years**) · **EARAS** (Kerala, Odisha, WB + Arunachal, Nagaland, Sikkim, Tripura) · **ICS** (supervisors check **4 clusters × 5 survey numbers**).
## Cost of cultivation
! Scheme since **1970-71**, run by DES through agricultural universities, about **25 crops**. Data used for **CACP → MSP**, NAS and the marketed surplus ratio.
- Design: agro-climatic zones → **tehsil (PPSWR)** → **village (PPSWR)** → **10 holdings** (2 from each of 5 size classes by SRSWOR). Uses the **cost accounting** method.
## FASAL & others
! FASAL partners: **IMD** (agromet) · **IEG** (econometric, **F0/F1**; monthly for 13 crops) · **SAC Ahmedabad** (RS methods; **8 of 11** crops transferred) · **NRSC** (satellite data). **MNCFC** set up **23 Apr 2012**.
- Small area estimation: **synthetic (scaling-down)** method. It assumes the small area behaves like the large one; if not, the estimate is biased.
- DES indices: **area, production, yield** and **terms of trade** (agriculture vs non-agriculture).
- **FSI** set up **1981**; remote sensing since **1987**; **biennial** forest assessment. **CACP** recommends MSP.
- SASA by department: **Land Records** in Chhattisgarh, HP, MP; **DES** in AP, Assam, Bihar, J&K, Karnataka, Kerala, Odisha, Rajasthan, TN; **Agriculture** in the rest.
