## CPI-IW (Labour Bureau)
! Base **2016** (**88 centres**; earlier 2001 = 78 centres; linking factor 2.88). Weights from **WCFIES**.
! Working class family: in the centre; one **manual** worker in **7 sectors** (factories, mines, plantations, railways, public motor transport, electricity, ports and docks; **not construction**); **≥ 50%** of income from manual work.
- Uses: **DA**, minimum wages, **urban poverty lines**, real-wage deflator, contract escalation.
- History: first series base **1960 = 100**, **50 centres**, from **Aug 1968** (FLS 1958-59).
## CPI-AL / RL (Labour Bureau)
! Base **1986-87**. Weights from the **NSS 38th round (1983)**. CPI-AL started **Sept 1964**. AL and RL use the **same prices** but **different weights**.
- Agricultural labour household = **≥ 50%** of income from agricultural manual labour, and it is a subset of rural labour households.
- Used for **agricultural minimum wages** and **rural poverty lines**.
## CPI (R/U/C) (NSO-PSD)
! Started **Jan 2011 (base 2010)**. Base **2012** series released from **Jan 2015**. Weights from the **68th round CES (2011-12)**.
! Prices from **1114 urban markets + 1181 villages**, weekly roster (FOD). Released on the **12th**.
! CPI-C group weights: **Food & Beverages 45.86** > Misc 28.32 > **Housing 10.07 (urban only)** > Fuel 6.84 > Clothing 6.53 > **Pan & Tobacco 2.38**. Food items (CFPI) = **39.06**.
- Price reference year **2012** vs weight reference year **2011-12**. Inflation is point-to-point (y-o-y). The **RBI targets CPI-C at 4 ± 2%**.
## CPI-UNME (discontinued)
- Run by the CSO from **Jan 1961**. Last base **1984-85**; family living survey **1982-83** in **59 centres**. Released on the 25th.
! The NSC (**Feb 2008**) discontinued it from **Apr 2008**. A linked series (CPI-IW sub-group indices with UNME weights) ran **Apr 2008 – Dec 2010**.
## WPI (OEA, DPIIT)
! Base **2011-12**, **697 items** (564 manufactured, 117 primary, 16 fuel). Released on the **14th** with a **2-week lag**. **No services, no indirect taxes**.
! Weights: **Manufactured 64.23 / Primary 22.62 / Fuel & Power 13.15**. **Food = 24.38** (15.26 + 9.12).
- Weights are based on **net traded value** = output + imports − exports. Prices are taken at ex-factory, ex-mine and mandi level.
