## ASI
! Run by the **IS Wing, DQAD (Kolkata)**. Covers factories under **2m(i) and 2m(ii)**, **bidi and cigar** units, repair, gas, water and cold storage. Conducted under the **CoS Act 2008 (amended 2017) + Rules 2011**.
! Frame = **Chief Inspector of Factories** lists. **Part I** is processed by the IS Wing; **Part II** (absenteeism, turnover, man-hours) by the **Labour Bureau**.
- Reference period = **financial year**. Survey period **Nov – Jul**. Uses the **establishment** approach.
- Unit of enumeration: **factory** (manufacturing) · **workshop** (repair) · **undertaking or licensee** (utilities) · **establishment** (bidi).
! Sample strata = **State × District × Sector × 3-digit NIC**; circular systematic, minimum **4 units** in **4 sub-samples**. **Sub-samples 1 & 3 go to FOD, 2 & 4 to the State**. Census units are in both samples.
## Economic Census & FuS
! ECs: **1977, 1980, 1990, 1998, 2005, 2013, 2019-20**. The scheme was launched by the CSO in **1976**.
- The EC covers all non-farm establishments of any size, formal and informal. It builds the frame for surveys and the **National Business Register**.
- **ASI** is record-based (registered units). **FuS/ASUSE** is interview-based (unregistered units), using the EC village and block frame.
## IIP & core industries
! IIP: base **2011-12**, **Laspeyres**, **ESD**. Values are deflated by WPI. **14 source agencies**. Notes: released on the **12th**, 6-week lag.
! Sector weights: **Manufacturing 77.633** (DPIIT) · **Mining 14.373** (**IBM Nagpur**) · **Electricity 7.994** (**CEA**).
- Use-based weights: **Primary 34** > Intermediate 17 > Consumer non-durables 15 > Consumer durables 13 > Infrastructure 12 > **Capital 8**.
! Eight core industries = **40.27%** of IIP. The ICI is released by **OEA**. Order of weights: **Refinery 28.04 > Electricity 19.85 > Steel 17.92 > Coal 10.33** … **Fertilisers 2.63** (lowest).
## ASI vs IIP growth
- IIP has a **fixed basket** while ASI captures new items; IIP has a **smaller sample**; IIP measures **volume** while ASI measures **value added**; **IIP units are larger**, so IIP growth is lower.
