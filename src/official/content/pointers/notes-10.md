# Topic 10 · Labour Statistics
@source Notes 10 · pp. 1-9
@group notes
! **Labour Bureau (Shimla/Chandigarh), MoLE**. Its scheme is **LESS**: CPI-IW, RLE and CPI-AL/RL, ASI labour part, OWS, QES, WRI, Minimum Wages evaluation, and others.
- CPI-IW uses: DA, retail inflation, real-wage deflator, **urban poverty line**, contract escalation, interest rates, wage/price/tax policy.
- Rural labour household = income mainly from **wage-paid manual labour** (365 days). Agricultural labour household = **≥ 50%** of income from agricultural manual labour.
- Socio-Economic Survey Division: SC/ST workers (urban), unorganised workers, women workers, **Minimum Wages Act 1948** evaluation.
## Surveys
! First **QES** in **Jan 2009** (reference Oct-Dec 2008) to measure the effect of the **global slowdown**. **AQEES** = **QES (10+ workers)** + **AFES (≤ 9 workers)** across **9 sectors**.
- QES measures the **demand side** and PLFS the **supply side**. QES misses over 90% of informal workers.
- AEUS: 1st in **2010** (reference Apr 2009 – Mar 2010), then 2012, 2013, 2015, 2016.
- ASI labour part: Labour Bureau handles **Block E of Part I + Part II**. Vol I covers employment and labour cost; Vol II adds absenteeism and turnover. The **DDG, FOD** is the Statistics Authority.
## Wages
! **OWS** first held **1958-59**. FSU = registered factories, SSU = workers. Precision target: within **5% at 95%** confidence.
! **WRI** base **2016** (old base **1963-65**), compiled since **1969**. Covers **37 industries** (30 manufacturing, 4 mining, 3 plantation). Weights **82.57 / 11.23 / 6.20**. Data for **January and July**, manual workers only, Laspeyres. Used for minimum wages and the national floor wage.
## New surveys
! **All-India Survey on Domestic Workers**: first of its kind. Covers **37 States/UTs, 742 districts, 12,766 FSUs, 1.5 lakh households**. It estimates live-in and live-out workers.
- The five Labour Bureau surveys: **Domestic Workers, Migrant Workers, Professionals, Transport sector, AQEES**. PLFS is **not** one of them (it is NSO's).
- Contract Labour Survey: since **1955**.
