## History & base year
! First official estimates: **National Income Committee (1949)**, chaired by **P.C. Mahalanobis**.
! Base years were revised decennially to years ending in "1" (the Census workforce), up to the **1980-81** series. From the **1993-94** series they were revised quinquennially (NSSO EUS). **2009-10 was not normal** (global slowdown), so the base became **2011-12**, released **30 Jan 2015**, following **SNA 2008** (UN, WB, IMF, OECD, EU).
- The NSC recommends rebasing economic indices **at least every 5 years**.
## Formulae
! **GVA(bp) = output(bp) − IC(pp)**. Income side: **CE + OS/MI + CFC + production taxes − production subsidies**.
! **GDP = Σ GVA(bp) + product taxes − product subsidies**. **GNI = GDP + net primary income from RoW**. **NNI = GNI − CFC**. **NNDI = NNI + net other current transfers**. **GNDI = NNDI + CFC**.
- **GCF = GFCF + CIS + valuables** (expenditure side) = **gross saving + net capital inflow** (financing side). The flow-of-funds estimate is treated as firmer. GCF by industry excludes valuables.
- GDI(Government) = GFCE + government gross saving. GDI(Households) = GNDI − GDI(Govt) − corporate saving.
! **Production taxes** don't depend on volume: land revenue, stamps and registration, profession tax. **Product taxes** are per unit: excise, GST/sales tax, customs. **Product subsidies**: food, fertiliser, petroleum. **Production subsidies**: railways, village and small industries.
- Basic price = factor cost + net production taxes. Market price = basic price + net product taxes.
## Calendar & methods
! Annual: **FAE (early Jan) → SAE (end Feb) → PE (end May) → FRE (Jan of the next year)**. Quarterly: quarter end **+ 2 months** (Q1 → end Aug).
- AE and quarterly estimates use the **benchmark-indicator** method (IIP, listed-company results, crop estimates, Government spending, services indicators). FRE uses budgets and company accounts.
- **PFCE and GFCF use commodity flow**. GFCE comes from budget documents. Exports and imports come from **Customs + BoP**.
## SUT & IOTT
! **SUT** (NAD): supply at **basic prices**, use at **purchasers' prices**. It is the best framework for current-price GDP.
! **IOTT**: first for **1968-69**; latest in the notes **2007-08**. It is a **commodity × industry** table at **factor cost**.
