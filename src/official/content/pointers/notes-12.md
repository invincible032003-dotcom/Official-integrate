# Topic 12 · Sample Registration System
@source Notes 12 · pp. 1-5
@group notes
! **SRS** (ORGI): **BR, DR, IMR** at **natural division** level for major States and State level for small States. Pilot **1964-65**, full scale **1969-70**.
! Dual record: **continuous enumeration** (part-time enumerators: anganwadi workers, teachers) + **half-yearly retrospective survey** (supervisor) → **matching** → field verification → **verbal autopsy**.
- **95% CI = estimate ± 2 SE**.
- Design: uni-stage stratified **SRSWOR**; **rural stratum II (large villages)** has two stages. Urban: **4 size strata** (<1 lakh, 1-5 lakh, ≥ 5 lakh, 4 metros; there were 6 before 2001). The unit is the **CEB**, and 3 sub-strata are formed by **female literacy**.
- SRS 2019 (notes): BR highest **Bihar**, lowest **A&N**; DR highest **Chhattisgarh**, lowest **Delhi**; IMR highest **MP**, lowest **Mizoram/Nagaland**.
## Formulae
! **GFR** = births ÷ women **15-49**. **GMFR** = births ÷ married women 15-49. **ASMFR** = births ÷ married women of the age group.
! **IMR** = infant deaths ÷ **live births** × 1000 = **NMR (< 29 days)** + **PNMR (29 days – < 1 year)**. **Early NMR** < 7 days; **late NMR** 7 – < 29 days.
! **PMR** = (still births + deaths < 7 days) ÷ (**live births + still births**). **Still birth rate** uses the same denominator.
