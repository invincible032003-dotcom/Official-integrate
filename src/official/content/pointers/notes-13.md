## Population Census
! First census **1872** (non-synchronous); first after Independence **1951**. **2011 = 15th census, 7th after Independence**. The next was notified as **Census 2027**.
- Per the notes: **de facto until 1931, de jure since 1941**. India uses the **canvasser** method (the householder method is not used because of low literacy).
! Permanent census machinery since **1949** (RGI, ex officio Census Commissioner, **MHA**). **R.A. Gopalaswami** ran 1951 and **C. Chandramouli** ran 2011.
- Two phases: **houselisting & housing census**, then **population enumeration** 6-8 months later.
- Legal basis: **Census Act 1948**, Census Rules **1990**, Citizenship Act **1955**, RBD Act **1969**. **NPR** under the Citizenship Rules **2003** (first 2010, updated 2015).
## CRS & RBD Act
! Register births and deaths at the **place of occurrence** within **21 days**. Delayed registration is under **Section 13**. A **Chief Registrar** is the authority in each State.
! **RBD (Amendment) Act 2023** (in force 1 Oct 2023): a **national database** with the RGI, and the **birth certificate** becomes the proof of date and place of birth (admissions, DL, voter list, jobs, passport).
- **CRS** = continuous, permanent, compulsory, universal. It is the **only source of district-level** vital rates. Records are **legal documents first**, statistics second.
- SRS gives State-level rates. The Census is decennial, so its rates are centred on mid-decade.
- ORGI publications: Vital Statistics (CRS) annual, SRS Bulletin, MCCD report. The Linguistic Survey is done by the **Language Division, ORGI**.
- Mother tongue = the language spoken to the person by the mother in childhood; if she died, the language mainly spoken at home.
