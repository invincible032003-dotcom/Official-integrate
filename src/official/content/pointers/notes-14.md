! Nodal agency **IIPS Mumbai**. Funded by MoHFW, **USAID, BMGF, UNICEF, UNFPA**.
! Rounds: **1992-93, 1998-99, 2005-06, 2015-16, 2019-21** (the notes print NFHS-4 as 2014-15).
! NFHS-5 design: **stratified two-stage**, Census 2011 frame. PSU = village or CEB. PSUs with **< 40 households** are linked; those with **≥ 300 households** are segmented into 100-150 household segments and **2** are chosen. **22 households** per cluster.
- Rural: 3 (size) × 2 (SC/ST) = **6 substrata**, sorted by **female literacy (6+)**. Urban CEBs are sorted by **SC/ST share**. Selection is PPS systematic.
! Tables use the **de facto** population (those who slept there last night). **De jure** = usual residents.
- Sex ratio **991 → 1020** needs care: single male migrants are not counted as usual residents, and visitors (often women at parental homes) are counted.
