# Topic 15 · Trade Statistics
@source Notes 15 · pp. 1-3
@group notes
! **DGCI&S, Kolkata** (Ministry of Commerce) compiles trade data from customs **Daily Trade Returns**, following **IMTS Rev.2 (UNSD)**.
! Source documents: **Shipping Bills (exports)** and **Bills of Entry (imports)**.
! Who does what: **DGFT** licensing · **DGCI&S** balance of trade · **RBI** balance of payments (BPM6).
! Release: quick estimates by the **15th** → principal commodities within **30 days** → 8-digit detail within **60 days**.
- Ancillary statistics: inter-State movement by rail, river and air (Zonal Railways, **CIWTC**, Indian Airlines); customs and excise revenue (**Directorate of Data Management**); coastal cargo (**12 maritime blocks**; zones Kolkata, Chennai, Kochi, Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Goa).
! Foreign trade indices: began **1944 (base 1938-39)**; now **base 2012-13**, all **8-digit ITC-HS** items. **Unit value index = Laspeyres (fixed base)**; **quantity index = value index ÷ UVI**. Published monthly, quarterly and yearly.
