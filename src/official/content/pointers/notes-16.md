# Topic 16 · PLFS
@source Notes 16 · pp. 1-5
@group notes
! Launched **April 2017** by NSO. **Quarterly**: urban, **CWS** (WPR, LFPR, UR). **Annual**: rural + urban, **usual status (ps+ss) + CWS**. A revamp from **Jan 2025** added monthly and rural quarterly estimates.
! Urban uses a **rotational panel**: 1 first visit + 3 revisits, with **75%** of FSUs matched between consecutive quarters. Rural covers **25%** of FSUs each quarter. FSU = UFS block (urban) or village (rural).
- July-Sep 2021 urban sample: **5,676 FSUs, 44,272 households, 1,71,405 persons**.
## Concepts
! **LFPR** = (employed + unemployed) ÷ population. **WPR** = employed ÷ population. **UR** = unemployed ÷ **labour force**. So **UR = 1 − WPR/LFPR**.
! **ps** = major time over **365 days**. **ss** = **30+ days** of economic activity within those 365 days. **CWS** = 7 days, **1 hour on any day** makes a person employed. Priority: **working > seeking > out of the labour force**.
! **CDS**: **≥ 4 hours** = full day; **1-4 hours** = half day; intensities **1.0 / 0.5**. With no work, **≥ 4 hours** seeking = unemployed for the full day.
- Employed = self-employed + regular wage/salaried + casual labour. Unpaid helpers count as employed.
