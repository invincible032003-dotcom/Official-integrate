# Topic 17 · SDGs & Principles of Official Statistics
@source Notes 17 · pp. 1-5
@group notes
! **2030 Agenda** adopted by the **70th UNGA on 25 Sep 2015**; in force **1 Jan 2016**. **17 goals, 169 targets, about 232 indicators**. Not legally binding.
- MDGs (2000): **8 goals, 18 targets**. SDGs are universal and cover economic, social and environmental dimensions.
! **NITI Aayog** oversees implementation (SDG India Index). **MoSPI** builds the **NIF** (306 indicators initially) and the dashboard.
- SDGs 12 → 17: consumption, **climate (13)**, life below water (14), life on land (15), peace and institutions (16), partnerships (17).
## SDDS
! India subscribes to the IMF **SDDS** (since 1996). **Metadata go on the DSBB**; data go on the **NSDP**, which is linked to it. Per the notes, **71 countries** are on SDDS and **14 on SDDS Plus**.
- UNSD publications: **Statistical Yearbook, World Statistics Pocketbook, Monthly Bulletin of Statistics**. The UN recommends revising index bases **every 5 years**.
## Fundamental Principles (UNGA 68/261, 29 Jan 2014)
! **1** Relevance, impartiality, equal access · **2** Professional standards · **3** Accountability & transparency · **4** Prevention of misuse · **5** Sources (quality, timeliness, cost, burden) · **6** Confidentiality · **7** Legislation · **8** National coordination · **9** International standards · **10** International cooperation.
- History: UNSC adopted them in **1994**, ECOSOC in **2013**, UNGA in **2014**. India adopted them by Cabinet in **2016**.
