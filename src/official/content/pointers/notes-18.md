# Topic 18 · Collection of Statistics Act & Rules
@source Notes 18 · pp. 1-5
@group notes
! Timeline: enacted **7 Jan 2009** (Act 7 of 2009), gazette **9 Jan 2009**, in force **11 Jun 2010**, Rules **16 May 2011**, corrigendum **8 Sep 2011**. The **2017 amendment** extended it to J&K.
! Constitutional basis: **Union 94 + Concurrent 45**; no State List entry.
- "Appropriate Government" covers all **three tiers**: Centre, State/UT and local bodies (Panchayat, Municipality).
- A **Section 3** notification lists subject, purpose, area, method, informant type, period, reference period, language, obligations and records. Copies go to the **Central and State nodal officers**.
- The **Statistics Officer** decides the mode (interview, form, notice, phone, fax, email, electronic, or mixed).
- Informants (Sections **2(c) and 5**) include firms and companies through their representatives. The **head of family** answers for family members and the **head of an institution** for its inmates. Being picked in a sample is **no defence** for refusing.
! Right of entry **10:00-17:00** (or a mutually convenient time), into the visitor area, carrying a photo ID.
## Penalties
! **Section 15(1)** refusal or non-production: **₹1,000** (company **₹5,000**). **Section 15(2)**: a further fine for each day of default beyond **14 days** after conviction.
! **Section 16** false statement: up to **6 months** or ₹1,000 / ₹5,000. **Section 17** destroying or defacing schedules: up to **6 months** or **₹2,000 / ₹10,000**.
- Voluntary surveys suffer refusals, false answers, fear of tax misuse and no confidentiality guarantee. The Act fixes all four.
