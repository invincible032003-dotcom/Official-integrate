# Topic 19 · Miscellaneous Statistics
@source Notes 19 · pp. 1-5
@group notes
! **Payroll reporting**: from **April 2018**, covering data from **Sept 2017**, using **EPF, ESI and NPS** subscribers (formal sector). Released around the **25th** of each month.
- Literacy: international norm **15+**; Indian Census effective literacy **7+**.
- **MISO** (Army HQ) is the main statistical organisation of the Ministry of Defence.
! Base-year criteria: close to the income and expenditure survey; reliable prices available; a **normal year**; the same base for all centres.
- DQAD's six DPCs: Delhi, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Ahmedabad, **Giridih**, Nagpur.
## Population projections
! **Cohort component** method for **21 States + 1 UT**. The **7 NE States** (excluding Assam) are projected as one block. **Ladakh** is a residual. **Goa and the other UTs** use the **mathematical** method. Data: Census 2011 + SRS.
## Unorganised & informal sector
! Unorganised GVA is estimated by the **labour input method** (workers × GVA per worker, benchmark-indicator). Unorganised manufacturing is about **one-third** of manufacturing GDP; unorganised services about **30%** of GDP.
- Organised services = registered under the **Companies or Societies Act** (education: Government-funded salaries). Organised manufacturing = **Factories Act**.
! **15th ICLS (1993)** defined the informal sector. **NCEUS** set up in **2004** (Arjun Sengupta). **Delhi Group** set up in **1997** for informal-sector statistics.
- UN city groups: Delhi (informal sector), Ottawa (prices), Voorburg (services), Wiesbaden (business registers), Rio (poverty), London (environment).
## IIP use-based classes
! **Primary goods** replaced "basic goods": ores, minerals, **fuels, electricity**. **Capital goods**: boilers, **tractors, commercial vehicles**, machinery. **Infrastructure**: **cement**, paints, bricks, cables. **Intermediate**: yarn, plywood, steel tubes. **Consumer durables**: cars, two-wheelers, **tyres, gold jewellery**. **Non-durables**: milk, rice, sugar, tea.
