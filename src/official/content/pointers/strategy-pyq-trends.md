# What UPSC asks: Official Statistics PYQ trends (2018-2026)
@source Analysis of the 165 Official Statistics PYQs in this dashboard
@group strategy
## Frequency by topic (165 PYQs)
! **Index numbers 36** · **Statistical system 34** · **Nature of official statistics 25** · **Sector-wise statistics 22** · **Census & vital statistics 18** · **Socio-economic indicators 15** · **National accounts 15**. The top three give over half the marks.
- 15-20 Official Statistics questions appear each year, so this unit alone is worth roughly **40-50 marks** of Paper II.
## Repeated themes
! **Who compiles which index** (CPI-IW/AL/RL = Labour Bureau; CPI-R/U/C = NSO; WPI = OEA) appears almost every year. Also **base years**, **Fisher's tests**, **L vs P weights**, **real wage and purchasing power** numericals, and **Fisher numericals** from Σp·q sums.
! **Statistical system**: NSC (2005 resolution, not statutory; part-time chair + 4 members + ex officio; CSI as Secretary), CSO/NSSO divisions, the Constitution (**Union 94 + Concurrent 45**; Census = Union 69), the CoS Act (all three tiers; economic, demographic, social, scientific, environmental), subject-wise decentralisation.
! **Nature**: FPOS (10 principles; spotting a fake principle), **public good**, **metadata**, NSS design (**two-stage stratified**, **household** as USU, **July-June**), PLFS **rotational panel**, **administrative vs survey** data, **disclosure risk**.
- **Census & vital statistics**: reference moment **00:00 on 1 March**; Census 2027 is the **16th** census (8th after Independence), digital, with caste; ORGI functions; GFR/NMR/PNMR formulae; Census House; houselisting items; Census tables.
- **Sector-wise**: MSP (CACP; not a DBT), Agriculture Census (5-yearly, operational holding, MoA&FW, 2021-22), ASI as the principal industrial source, Livestock Census (5-yearly, domesticated animals only), PLFS objectives, the EC.
- **Socio-economic**: SDGs (17 goals, UN, in force 1 January 2016, not mandatory), HDI (no IMR, no gender equality), PQLI (no income), GHI (100-point scale, 0 is best), TUS 2019, natural capital, high-frequency indicators.
- **National accounts**: GVA = output − IC; national income = **NNI**; residence vs nationality; primary sector includes mining; secondary = manufacturing + utilities + construction; PFCE is the largest share; quarterly growth is year-on-year.
## Traps UPSC uses
- The options **"statutory / constitutional"** for the NSC; **"every ten years by law"** for the Census; **"all of the above"** for the Constitution Lists; **CPI-AL base 2015** (it is **1986-87**); **"NSSO"** as the CPI-AL compiler (it is the **Labour Bureau**); a national accounts base of **"2023-24"** (it is **2022-23**).
- Odd-one-out distractors: **NATO**, "**regulated use of AI**" as an SDG, "**pricing and advertisement**" as an FPOS principle, "**market borrowings**" as a way to finance statistics, "**Labour Census**".
