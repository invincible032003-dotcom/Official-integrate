## Question-type tactics
! **Statement questions**: verify each statement independently. "Only", "all" and "exclusively" usually make a statement false.
! **Match questions**: lock the one pair you are sure of, then eliminate options.
- **Assertion-Reason**: first judge A and R separately. Then ask whether R answers "why A?".
- **Numericals**: write the formula before substituting. UR divides by the **labour force**; IMR divides by **live births**; PMR adds still births to the denominator.
- Agency questions: find the **compiling agency** first (Labour Bureau, OEA, NSO, RBI, ORGI, DGCI&S). That usually settles it.
